B · Welcome Question Cards (C1 mingle)

One set per 12 learners (print two sets for a bigger class). Each learner takes one card of each color. They ask one question per partner, answer the partner's question, swap cards, and find a new partner. The small line is an extra question for stronger learners. Every card works with a new identity. None asks about the home country, age, family or migration, on purpose.

BE
Are you a morning person or a night person?
+ What time are you sleepy?
BE
What's your favorite food? Is it sweet, salty or spicy?
+ Is it easy to cook?
BE
Who is your favorite singer or actor?
+ Why is he / she great?
BE
What's your favorite day of the week? Why?
+ Is today a good day?
DO
What do you do on Sundays?
+ Do you get up early on Sunday?
DO
Do you like coffee or tea? How many cups do you drink?
+ When do you drink it?
DO
How do you come to class? (bus, car, walk…)
+ How long does it take?
DO
Tell me about a friend. What does he / she do? What does he / she like?
+ Where do you meet?
CAN
What can you cook?
+ Can you cook it for 20 people?
CAN
Can you swim? Can you drive? Can you ride a bike?
+ Who taught you?
CAN
What can you do well?
+ What can't you do… yet?
CAN
Can you play an instrument or a sport?
+ Can you teach me?

B · A1 Diagnostic Grid (teacher only · C1)

Listen during the welcome mingle and the report. Mark ✓ easy and accurate · ~ understands, some errors · ✗ not yet. Write one real example in Notes. Keep this page private; compare it with the Progress Test in C13.

NameBE
I'm / she's / Are you…?		PRESENT SIMPLE
I work · Do you…? · she works		CAN
I can / Can you…?		Notes (an example I heard)
Listen for

BE: "I tired" / "She teacher" (no be) · "I have 30 years" · "Are you…?" word order. PRESENT SIMPLE: "I no like" · "You like coffee?" (no do) · "She like tea" (no -s) in the report. CAN: "I can to swim" · "I can swimming" · "Can you to…?"

Mostly ✓/~ = ready for A2. Many ~ in BE = go slowly with was/were today. ✗ in two or three columns = a private 5-minute oral placement interview this week (toolkit), and extra support from Day 1.

C · New Identity Cards (1.1 · 1.2 · 1.3)

Any learner may use a card instead of their real life, in any task: no reason needed. In C1 use the Yesterday part (was / were). In C2 retell the Last weekend part with regular verbs only. In C3 use the whole weekend in the mingle. A and B cards in a pair share two things (for your monitoring, see the bottom of the page).

D · Listening & Dictation Scripts (teacher reads aloud)

Read at a natural but slow speed, with pauses. Read each script twice: once for gist, once to check. Use natural weak forms (I w'z tired) the second time. The 🔊 buttons in the online lessons give learners extra listening at home.

1.1-A · How was your weekend? (Workbook 1.1, Ex 3: true or false · two voices)

Nora: Hi, Sam! How was your weekend? · Sam: It was OK. On Saturday I was at work. It was very busy. There were a lot of customers. · Nora: Oh no! Were you tired? · Sam: Yes, I was! But Sunday was great. My brother and his kids were at my apartment. We were in the park all afternoon. It was sunny and warm. · Nora: Nice! · Sam: And you? Were you at home? · Nora: No, I wasn't. I was at my sister's house in the mountains. It was beautiful, but the weather wasn't good. It was cold and rainy!

Answers: 1 T · 2 F (very busy) · 3 T · 4 T · 5 F (at her sister's house) · 6 T.

1.1-B · Dictation (optional; read each sentence 3 times · literacy support: sentences 1–2)

1. I was at home last night. · 2. We were very tired. · 3. Were you at work yesterday? · 4. There were a lot of people at the market. · 5. The movie wasn't very good.

1.2-A · The -ed sound: listen and show (1 finger = /t/, 2 = /d/, 3 = /ɪd/)

worked · played · wanted · cooked · cleaned · visited · watched · arrived · needed · walked · listened · started · talked · stayed · decided

Answers: worked 1 · played 2 · wanted 3 · cooked 1 · cleaned 2 · visited 3 · watched 1 · arrived 2 · needed 3 · walked 1 · listened 2 · started 3 · talked 1 · stayed 2 · decided 3. Make /ɪd/ very clear; accept a small /t/ vs /d/ blur.

1.2-B · Rosa's day (Workbook 1.2, Ex 3: number the pictures)

Hi, I'm Rosa. Yesterday was a long day! I started work at seven in the morning. I worked at the hospital until three. After work, I walked to the market, and I shopped for vegetables. Then I went home, and I cooked dinner for my family. After dinner, my son helped me, and we cleaned the kitchen. At eight o'clock, I called my sister. We talked for an hour! Then I watched TV and listened to some music. I stayed up late. It was a good day.

Answers: b hospital 1 · d market 2 · e cook 3 · f kitchen 4 · c phone 5 · a TV 6. went is the only irregular verb: don't stop for it.

1.3-A · Market day (Workbook 1.3, Ex 4: true or false)

Last Saturday, my friend Dina and I went to the city market. We took the bus at nine, and we arrived at ten. The market was very busy. There were a lot of people! I bought some fruit and a new scarf. Dina didn't buy anything. She only looked! At noon we ate lunch at a small restaurant. I had fish and rice, and Dina had a salad. We drank mango juice. Then we saw a street musician. He sang and played the guitar. We didn't go home until six. It was a great day!

Answers: 1 T · 2 F (the bus) · 3 T · 4 F (she didn't buy anything) · 5 F (a small restaurant) · 6 T. Stretch: "Which verbs are irregular?" (went, took, bought, ate, had, drank, saw, sang).

1.3-B · Did you…? Listen and answer (Workbook 1.3, Ex 3b: write a short answer about you)

1. Did you have breakfast today? · 2. Did you come to class by bus? · 3. Did you watch TV last night? · 4. Did you cook yesterday? · 5. What did you eat for dinner last night?

Answers are personal: Yes, I did. / No, I didn't. For 5, a short answer ("Rice and beans.") is fine. Listen for "Yes, I ate."

F · The -ed Sound: Sort Cards (1.2)

One set per group. Cut the cards and the answer strip. Put the three header cards on the table (the fourth card "?" is for "not sure yet"). Learners take turns: take a card, say it out loud with a hand on the throat, and put it under /t/, /d/ or /ɪd/. The group agrees or says "Say it again!"

H · Game — Irregular-Verb Memory (1.3)

One set per group of 3–4, on card stock (learners must not see through the cards). Cut on the dashed lines. White = base verb · blue = past form · amber = bonus pairs for stronger groups. Shuffle; place face down in rows. Turn over two and say both words. A match = base + past. To keep the pair, say a past sentence: "Last weekend I bought shoes." After the game, turn your pairs into negatives: "I didn't buy shoes."

The cards are printed in pairs so you can check sets quickly after class; always shuffle before play. Easier: 8 pairs, face up, as a matching race. I · Mingle — What Did You Do Last Weekend? (C3)

Photocopy one per learner. Part 1: learners stand, walk, and ask four classmates the three questions; notes only (2–3 words). Part 2: "Find someone who…": ask Did you…? If the answer is "Yes, I did," write the name. At the end, three learners tell the class about one person with he / she + past. Real weekend, a new identity card (Set C), or a dream weekend: all are fine.
