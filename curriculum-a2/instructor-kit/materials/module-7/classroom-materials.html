E · Problems & Returns — Carousel Cards (7.2)

Groups of 4: one server, one sales clerk, two customers. Customers take a card, go to the right staff member, and solve the problem in 3 steps (soften → explain → ask). Then take a new card. After 6 minutes, customers and staff swap. Staff always answer: sorry → action.

Staff · server

🍽️ You work at Rosa's Kitchen

The kitchen can make any dish again (10 minutes). If a customer waits a long time, you can offer a free drink or dessert.

  • "I'm so sorry about that."
  • "I'll bring you a new one right away."
  • "Let me check with the kitchen."
  • "You're right. I'll fix the check."
  • "Would you like separate checks?"
Staff · sales clerk

🛍️ You work at City Clothes

Store policy: With a receipt, within 30 days: refund or exchange. No receipt: exchange or store credit only. No refund.

  • "Is there something wrong with it?"
  • "I'm sorry about that. Do you have the receipt?"
  • "Would you like a refund or an exchange?"
  • "I'm afraid we can't give a refund without a receipt, but…"

G · Pharmacy — Pharmacist Card (7.3)

Same pairs, step 2. The patient asks "Do you have something for…?" and "How often should I take it?" The pharmacist answers from the shelf and gives advice with should, 'd better and Why don't you…? Then swap.

H · Listening Scripts (teacher reads aloud)

Read at a natural but slow speed, with pauses. Read each script twice: once for gist, once to check. Two voices? Change your voice a little, or ask a confident learner to read the second part. The 🔊 buttons in the online lessons give learners extra listening at home.

I · Game — Around Town (Module 7 review)

Groups of 3–4, one board, one die, a coin for each player. Roll and move. Do the task on your square. The group decides: "Good English? Polite?" If yes, stay. If no, go back to your last square. Yellow squares move you. The first player to FINISH wins. Health squares are always a pretend patient. Use it for C22 fast finishers or as a 10-minute C23 warmer.

Teacher note

Walk around and listen for the three big skills of the module: the polite opener (Excuse me… / I'd like… / Could I…?), the complaint softener (I'm afraid there's a problem with…) and advice + base verb (You should rest). Note one great sentence per group and read them out at the end. No dice? Learners flip a coin: heads = 1 square, tails = 2.
