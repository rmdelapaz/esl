Welcome to Everyday English! 👋

Hello, and welcome! We are very happy you are here. 😊
You already know some English. In this class you will learn to tell stories about your life, make plans, and handle real situations: at the station, in a restaurant or a store, and at the doctor's.

Some of you finished our A1 class. Some of you are new. Everyone is welcome here.

🤝 Our class

  • 🕐Please come on time. Class starts with a speaking game!
  • 📵Please put your phone on silent. We use phones only for class activities.
  • 🗣️Speak English as much as you can. Mistakes are OK. They help you learn.
  • 👂Listen to your classmates, and give everyone time to speak.
  • 💛Be kind. We all come from different places, and we are all learning.
  • 🙋Don't understand? That's normal. Ask! Questions help everyone.
  • 📩If you can't come, please tell your teacher. Then do the online lesson at home.
  • 🔒Your information is yours. When we talk about our lives, you can use a real or an imagined story. That's always OK.

🏠 At home: free online lessons

📱 Everyday English (A2): free!

Every class has an online lesson with the same words and grammar. It is your homework, and it helps you catch up if you miss a class. You can use a phone, a tablet, or a computer.

rays-esl.netlify.app/curriculum-a2/
  1. Open the website, or scan the QR code with your phone camera. 📷
  2. Click today's lesson. Your teacher will tell you the number (for example, Lesson 1.2).
  3. Click the 🔊 buttons. Listen to the words and the conversations, then say them out loud. 🗣️ (No sound? Turn up the volume, or try the Chrome browser. You can choose a US or UK voice and slow it down.)
  4. Do the exercises and the quiz. ✔️ Then try the project: tell your story out loud, or record yourself.

⏱ 20–30 minutes, 3 times a week, is a great plan. 🌟
