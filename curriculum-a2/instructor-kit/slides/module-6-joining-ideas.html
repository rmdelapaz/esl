6.1 · Order · will / might · if / when

Two orders. How sure are you?

If firstIf you study, you'll pass.
If secondYou'll pass if you study.
will ✅If you ask him, he'll help.
might 🤷If you ask him, he might help.
whenWhen I get home, I'll call you. (sure)
ifIf I get home early, I'll call you. (maybe)
SAY IT If it RAINS ↗ | we'll stay HOME ↘ · I'll /aɪl/ · we'll /wil/ · won't ≠ want
6.1 · Check the meaning

"If it rains, we'll stay home."

Is it raining now? (No / we don't know.)  ·  Past or future? (Future.)  ·  Is rain possible? (Yes.)
"If it rains, we might stay home." Sure? (No. Maybe.)
"When I get home, I'll call you." Will I get home? (Yes, of course.)
"If I will have time…" OK? (No! If I have time…)

Hiking on Saturday?

Maya: Do you want to go hiking on Saturday?

Leo: Maybe. If the weather is good, I'll come with you.

Maya: And if it rains?

6.2 · Check the meaning

"The man who lives next door is a doctor."

Which man? (The man next door.)  ·  Who or which for a person? (Who.)
"That's the café. We met there." One sentence? (That's the café where we met.)
"the woman who she works at the bank" OK? (No. who = she.)
"the book I bought" OK? (Yes: a new subject, I.) · "the man lives here" OK? (No: keep who.)
WATCH the man which ❌ · the book what ❌ · the town that I grew up ❌ → where

6.3 · Linking words · five jobs

Sew your ideas together 🧵

➕ andaddI made tea and sat down.
↔️ butcontrastI like tea, but I prefer coffee.
➡️ soresultI was tired, so I went to bed.
❓ becausereason (Why?)I went to bed because I was tired.
😮 althoughsurpriseAlthough it rained, we went out.
6.3 · because ↔ so · although ↔ but

Same story, two ways

because + reasonI went to bed because I was tired.
so + resultI was tired, so I went to bed.
butIt rained, but we went out.
althoughAlthough it rained, we went out.
"Why did you go to bed?" — "So I was tired"? (No! "Because I was tired.")
WATCH Although…, but ❌ · Because…, so ❌ · one linker for one link
6.3 · Sentence builder · groups of 3 · 12 min

Choppy → smooth

Choppy: I was hungry. I made a sandwich. It was good.

Smooth: I was hungry, so I made a sandwich, and it was good.

6.3 · Writing · 12 min

My paragraph ✍️

I live in Riverside. It's a small town near a river. It's quiet, but it's friendly. There's a big market on Saturday, so I go there every weekend and buy fresh fruit. I like my town because the people are kind. Although the winters are cold, the summers are beautiful.

How many sentences? (5–7.) · Your real job or town only? (No: real, a card, or imagined.) · "Although… but" OK? (No!) · First sentence? (The topic.)
