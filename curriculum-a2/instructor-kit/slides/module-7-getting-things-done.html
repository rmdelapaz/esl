7.1 · Model dialogue · the ticket office

A round-trip, please.

Priya: Hi. A round-trip to downtown, please.

Clerk: Sure. That's eight fifty.

Priya: What time is the next train?

Clerk: The next one leaves at ten fifteen, from platform three.

Priya: Platform three. Do I need to change?

Clerk: No, it's direct.

Round-trip: do I come back? (Yes.)  ·  One-way: do I come back on this ticket? (No.)  ·  Direct: do I change trains? (No.)

7.2 · Model dialogue · booking by phone

I'd like to book a table.

Host: Good afternoon, Rosa's Kitchen. How can I help you?

Caller: Hi. I'd like to book a table for four on Saturday, please.

Host: Sure. What time?

Caller: Seven thirty, if possible.

Host: I'm sorry, we're fully booked at seven thirty. We have a table at eight.

Caller: Eight is fine. Could we sit by the window?

Host: No problem. What name is it under?

7.2 · Grammar · too & enough

It's too small. It isn't big enough.

😟 too + adjIt's too expensive. The shoes are too small.
😟 + nountoo much salt · too many people
🙂 adj + enoughIt's big enough. · 😟 It isn't long enough.
enough + nounWe don't have enough chairs.
"It's too expensive." Can I buy it? (No.) A problem? (Yes.)  ·  "It's very expensive." Can I buy it? (Maybe!)
WATCH big enough ✅ · enough big ❌ · too many people ✅ · too much people ❌
7.2 · Polite problem-solving

I'm afraid there's a problem with…

😊 CUSTOMER
1 Soften: Excuse me. I'm afraid there's a problem with my soup.
2 Explain: It's cold.
3 Ask: Could I have a hot one, please?
🧑‍🍳 STAFF
Sorry: I'm so sorry about that.
Action: I'll bring you a new one right away.
Store: Would you like a refund or an exchange?
NOTE "I'm afraid…" = a soft start for bad news. You are not scared!

7.3 · Describing symptoms · how long?

I've had a cough since Monday.

I've got / I havea headache · a sore throat · a fever
I feeldizzy · sick · tired · I don't feel well.
hurtsMy back hurts. · It hurts when I swallow.
⏳ sincea point: since Monday · since last night · since 9 o'clock
⏳ fora length: for three days · for a week · for two hours
"…since Monday." When did it start? (Monday.) Now too? (Yes.)  ·  "since three days"? (No! for three days.)
7.3 · Giving advice · base verb

You should rest.

You should…a good ideaYou should drink lots of water.
You'd better…strong: important!You'd better see a doctor.
Why don't you…?a friendly ideaWhy don't you take a day off?
"You'd better see a doctor." Stronger than "should"? (Yes.) A problem if you don't? (Maybe.)  ·  "Why don't you…?" A real "why" question? (No. An idea.)
WATCH You should rest ✅ · You should to rest ❌ · Why don't you rest? ✅ · Why you don't rest? ❌
7.3 · Model dialogue · phoning the clinic

I'd like to make an appointment.

Ana: Hello, I'd like to make an appointment, please.

Recep.: Of course. What's it about?

Ana: I've had a sore throat and a fever since Monday. It hurts when I swallow.

Recep.: We have Friday at ten thirty.

Ana: Is there anything earlier? I feel quite sick.

Recep.: Dr. Lee can see you today at four fifteen.

Ana: Today at four fifteen. Thank you!

PHARMACY Do you have something for a sore throat? · How often should I take it?
