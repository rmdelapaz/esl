1.1 · Past of be · was / were

am / is → was · are → were

✅I / he / she / it was tired. · You / we / they were at home.
❌He wasn't at work. · They weren't late.
❓Were you at home? · Was it cold?
💬Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't. · Yes, they were. / No, they weren't.
Wh-How was your weekend? · Where were you?
WATCH you = always were · no did with was/were
1.1 · Check the meaning

"I was tired last night."

Now or before? (Before.)  ·  Am I tired now? (We don't know.)
"They were at the park." One person or more? (More. So: were.)
"You were right, Ana." One person. Was or were? (Were!)
"Were you at home?" Can we say "Did you were…?" (No!)
WATCH You were ✅ · You was ❌ · Did you were ❌

1.1 · Model dialogue

How was your weekend?

A: How was your weekend?

B: It was great! I was at the beach with my family.

A: Nice! Was the weather good?

B: Yes, it was sunny. There were a lot of people, but it wasn't too crowded.

A: Were your kids happy?

B: Very! They weren't tired at all. I was the tired one!

consonant + yy → iedstudy → studied · but play → played

1.3 · Group game · 12 min

Memory: go … went!

  • Cards face down. Turn over 2. Say both words.
  • Match = base + past: buy + bought
  • Keep it: say a sentence. "Last weekend I bought a cat!"
  • No match? Turn them back. Most pairs wins.
How many cards? (Two.)
Look or say? (Say them.)
To keep a pair? (A past sentence.)
No match? (Turn them back.)
1.3 · didn't · Did…? · one pattern for all

1 past word is enough!

✅I went to the market. · I watched TV.
❌I didn't go to the market. · I didn't watch TV.
❓Did you go? → Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
Wh-What did you do? · Where did you go? · Who did you meet?
"I didn't go." Which word shows the past? (didn't) So "went"? (No: go.)  ·  "at home": Did you…? or Were you…? (Were you at home?)
WATCH I didn't go ✅ · I didn't went ❌ · Did you went ❌
1.3 · Mingle · 12 min

What did you do last weekend?

A: What did you do last weekend?

B: I went to the market. I bought a shirt.

A: Did you have a good time?

B: Yes, I did! And you?

How many people? (Four.)
Real weekend only? (No: real, a card, or a dream.)
Long sentences? (No, notes.)
Tell the class: he / she…? (went, bought, didn't…)
