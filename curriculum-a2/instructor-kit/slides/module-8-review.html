8.1 · for & since · How long?

Started in the past. Still true now.

for ⏳ a lengthfor three years · for two weeks · for a long time
since 📅 a startsince 2021 · since Monday · since I was a child
TEST Can you count it? → for. A date, a day, an event? → since.

8.1 · just · already · yet

Done? Not done? When?

⚡ justa moment agoI've just finished.
✅ alreadydone (maybe early)She's already paid.
⏸️ yet ❓question · at the endHave you called yet?
⏸️ yet ❌not done (up to now) · at the endI haven't called yet.
ORDER have + just / already + past participle · … yet.
8.1 · Check the meaning

"I've lived here for five years."

Do I live here now? (Yes.)  ·  Is it finished? (No.)
"I lived in Texas for two years." Do I live in Texas now? (No. Finished → past simple.)
"I've just eaten." A minute ago or a year ago? (A minute ago.) Am I hungry? (No!)
"I haven't called her yet." Did I call? (No.) Am I going to call? (Probably, yes.)
CLUE yesterday · last week · in 2019 · ago → past simple  |  for · since · just · already · yet → present perfect
8.1 · Model dialogue

How long have you lived here?

A: Hi! How long have you lived in this neighborhood?

B: For about three years — since I started my new job. And you?

A: Since last summer. I've just moved into the apartment on the corner.

B: Nice! Have you found a good café yet?

A: Not yet. I haven't explored much.

B: I've already had breakfast, but I'd love a coffee. Let's go!

8.2 · Verb patterns · the two-column list

-ing or to?

verb + -ingverb + to + verb
enjoy — I enjoy dancing.want — I want to dance.
don't mind — I don't mind cooking.need — I need to cook.
finish — She finished eating.would like — She'd like to eat.
keep — He keeps talking.decide — He decided to talk.
suggest — They suggested leaving.hope · plan · promise · learn · try
BOTH like · love · hate: I love cooking. = I love to cook.
8.2 · Check the meaning

The FIRST verb decides.

"I don't mind waiting." Is waiting a problem? (No. It's OK.)
"He keeps forgetting his keys." One time or again and again? (Again and again.)
"I'd like to go home." = "I want to go home"? (Yes, but polite.) "I'd like going"? (No!)
WATCH I enjoy reading ✅ · I enjoy to read ❌ · I enjoy read ❌ · I want to read ✅ · I want reading ❌
8.2 · Model dialogue

What do you enjoy doing?

A: What do you enjoy doing on the weekend?

B: I love cooking, and I don't mind cleaning up after! This year I want to learn to bake bread.

A: Nice! I've decided to take a photography class. I hope to get better at it.

B: Do you plan to travel and take photos?

A: Yes — I'd like to visit the mountains in spring. I keep saving a little money every month.

8.2 · Group game · 15 min

Verb Pattern Race 🎲

  • Roll the die. Move your counter.
  • Make a true sentence with the verb: enjoy… on the weekend → "I enjoy sleeping late on the weekend."
  • The group checks: -ing or to? Wrong → back 1 square.
  • ★ = ask the player on your left.
A sentence or one verb? (A sentence.)
True? (Yes, or for your card.)
Wrong pattern? (Go back one.)
A star square? (Ask the person on my left.)
8.3 · Review · my A2 tense toolkit

Past → up to now → future

⬅️ PastI grew up in a small town. · I used to play soccer. · I was living with my family.
⏺️ Up to nowI've lived here for six years / since 2020. · I've just started a class.
➡️ FutureI'm going to study nursing. · I think I'll move. · If I pass, I'll celebrate.
TIME WORDS when I was young · in 2019 | for · since · just · yet | next year · one day · if…
8.3 · Listening · Maya's story

Where did you grow up?

Sam: Where did you grow up?

Maya: In a small town by the sea. My family used to run a little café.

Sam: How long have you lived in the city now?

Maya: For four years — since I started college. I've just finished my final project.

Sam: Have you found a job yet?

Maya: Not yet. But I'm going to apply to a design studio next week. If I get the job, I'll stay in the city.

8.3 · The task · story circles · 15 min

Tell your story

  • Groups of 3. Speak for about 2 minutes.
  • Look at your key words. Don't read!
  • Listeners: tick past · up to now · future.
  • Each listener asks one question.
  • It's OK to say "Pass!"
Your real life only? (No. Real, partly real, or a card.)
How many parts? (Three.)
Read your frame? (No, only key words.)
Listeners do what? (Tick and ask one question.)
