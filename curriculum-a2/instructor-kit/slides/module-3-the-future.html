Talking About the Future

My plans. My guesses. Our plans.

Classes C7–C9 · the map

Three futures

  • 3.1 My plans: I'm going to visit my sister. Look! It's going to rain.
  • 3.2 Guesses & quick decisions: I think it'll be sunny. I might go. I'll get it!
  • 3.3 Our plans: I'm meeting Sara on Friday. Let's… / How about…?
NOTE Your real plans, a future identity card, or a dream future: all are OK. You can say "Pass."

3.1 · be going to + base verb

Only the be verb changes

✅I'm going to visit my sister. · She's going to study. · We're going to travel.
❌I'm not going to work. · He isn't going to come. · They aren't going to wait.
❓Are you going to cook tonight? · What are you going to do?
💬Yes, I am. / No, I'm not. · Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
WATCH I going to ❌ · going to studying ❌ · going to to go ❌ · Yes, I'm ❌
3.1 · Two uses · check the meaning

🧠 A plan or 👀 a prediction?

🧠 "I'm going to visit my sister this weekend."
Now or future? (Future.)
Did I decide before? (Yes. It's a plan.)
👀 "Look at those black clouds! It's going to rain."
Is it raining now? (No.)
Can I see something? (Yes, clouds.)
Did I plan the rain? (No!)
SAME FORM The bus is full. We're going to be late! · Careful! You're going to drop that!
3.1 · Model dialogue

What are you going to do this weekend?

A: What are you going to do this weekend?

B: I'm going to visit my sister. We're going to see a movie on Saturday.

A: Nice! Are you going to stay the night?

B: Yes, I am. I'm not going to drive back so late. What about you?

A: I'm going to relax at home. Look at the forecast! It's going to rain all day Sunday.

3.2 · Check the meaning · say it

Plan → going to · Now → will

"I'm going to paint my room this weekend." Decided before or now? (Before. A plan.)
"Oh, you dropped your pen. I'll pick it up." Before or now? (Now.)
"I might go to the party." Yes or no? (Maybe! Not sure.)
SAY IT 👎 won't /woʊnt/ (like "don't") · 👍 want /wɑnt/ — "I won't go." ≠ "I want to go."
3.2 · Group game · 14 min

🔮 Crystal Ball · 🌦️ Weather Forecaster

  • Take a card. Roll the die: 1–2 won't · 3–4 might · 5–6 will
  • Make a prediction + a reason
  • The group: "I agree!" / "I don't think so. I think…"
  • "You" cards: kind and light only. Say "Pass!" any time.
First? (Take a card, roll.)
You roll a 3? (Might.) A 6? (Will.)
A kind or a sad prediction? (Kind!)
Don't like a card? ("Pass!")

3.3 · Suggestions & replies

Let's… / How about…?

+ base verbLet's go to the park. · Why don't we eat out? · Shall / Should we meet at 7?
+ -ing / nounHow about watching a movie? · How about Saturday?
inviteWould you like to come to dinner?

🙏I'd love to, but I'm working. · Maybe another time.
WATCH Let's to go ❌ · How about go ❌ · Why you don't come ❌ · just "No." 😬
3.3 · Model dialogue

Are you doing anything this weekend?

Mia: Are you doing anything this weekend?

Ben: Not really. Why?

Mia: How about seeing a movie? There's a new comedy.

Ben: Good idea! Shall we go on Saturday?

Mia: Saturday's hard. I'm meeting my sister. Why don't we go on Sunday?

Ben: Sure, that sounds great. So we're meeting at 6 on Sunday. See you then!

3.3 · Information gap · 20 min

Find a time to meet

  • A and B: don't show your diary
  • Ask: "Are you doing anything on Monday afternoon?"
  • Both free? Suggest: "Let's… / Why don't we…? / How about…?"
  • Confirm: "So we're meeting on … at … See you then!"
Show your diary? (No!)
What are you looking for? (A free time for both.)
A polite no? ("I'd love to, but…")
Last step? (Confirm the plan.)
