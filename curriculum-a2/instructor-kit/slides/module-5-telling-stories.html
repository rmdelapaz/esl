5.1 · Past continuous · was / were + -ing

Freeze a moment

✅I / he / she / it was cooking. · You / we / they were cooking.
❌I wasn't sleeping. · They weren't working.
❓Were you sleeping? · What were you doing at nine?
💬Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.
👯While I was reading, she was cooking.
SAY I wuz COOKing · Yes, I WAS!  NOT know · like · want · need · love → I knew
5.1 · Check the meaning

"At eight I was cooking dinner."

At eight, did I start cooking? (No, before.)  ·  Was it finished? (No.)  ·  In the middle? (Yes!)
"I cooked dinner." Finished? (Yes.)  ·  "I was cooking at eight." Do we know it's finished? (No.)
"While I was reading, she was cooking." One after the other, or at the same time? (Same time.)
WATCH I was cooking ✅ · I cooking ❌ · I was cook ❌ · They was ❌
5.1 · Model dialogue

What were you doing?

Ana: I called you at nine last night. What were you doing?

Ben: Sorry! I was taking a shower. What were you doing so late?

Ana: I was cooking a big dinner. My brother was helping me.

Ben: Was your sister helping too?

Ana: No, she wasn't. She was watching a movie while we were cooking!

5.1 · A mystery! 🔍

The Missing Birthday Cake 🎂

Last night, between 7 and 9 p.m., someone ate the birthday cake in the school kitchen. Only crumbs!

Two people say: "We were together all evening." Is it true?

GAME It's not real. The evening is invented. Have fun!
5.1 · Group task · 22 min

The Alibi Game

  • Groups of 4: 2 suspects + 2 detectives
  • Suspects: plan your evening together (7:00 → 9:00)
  • Detectives: write questions: What were you doing at…?
  • Ask each suspect alone. Compare the answers.
  • 3 differences? "You ate the cake!" 🎂
A real crime? (No! A game.)
Real or invented evening? (Invented.)
Suspects plan together or alone? (Together.)
Is the other suspect listening? (No!)
How many differences? (Three.)
5.2 · The long line and the dot

I was walking home when it started to rain.

Which started first, the walking or the rain? (The walking.)  ·  Was the rain starting long or short? (Short, sudden.)
"When the movie ended, we went home." Anything in progress? (No.) So both verbs are… (past simple.)
5.2 · when vs while

Two ways to tell it

WORD+ WHICH ACTIONEXAMPLE
whenthe short action (past simple)I was cooking when the phone rang.
whilethe long action (past continuous)While I was cooking, the phone rang.
COMMA When / While first → comma in the middle  PAST rang · fell · began · broke · saw · woke up · met · went out

5.2 · Model dialogue · telling what happened

You'll never guess what happened!

Sara: I was carrying two cups of coffee when I tripped on the step.

Tom: Oh no! Did you drop them?

Sara: Yes! While I was falling, the coffee went everywhere. It landed on a man's shoes!

Tom: That's terrible! Was he angry?

Sara: No, he laughed! He helped me up while I was saying sorry.

REACT Oh no! Are you OK? · Really? · That's so funny! · And then what happened?
5.2 · Pair task · 16 min

Something happened!

  • True, invented, or a story card
  • Scene ——— I was… The sun was…
  • Event • …when suddenly…
  • Then + the end
  • B reacts and asks: "And then what happened?"
Must it be true? (No.)
How do you start? (Set the scene.)
Tense for the sudden event? (Past simple.)
What does B do? (Listens, reacts, asks.)

5.3 · used to + base verb

Habits and states that changed

✅I / you / she / they used to walk to work.
❌I didn't use to like coffee.
❓Did you use to play soccer? — Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
🔄I used to walk to work, but now I drive.
Many times or one time? (Many.)  ·  True now? (No.)  ·  "Yesterday I used to see a movie"? (No! I saw.)
SAY "YOO-stuh"  WRITE did / didn't + use to (no -d!)
5.3 · Pair task · 20 min

Then & Now

  • Choose one: 🧒 my childhood · 💼 a job I used to have · 🎭 a character card
  • Notes: 4 THEN + 3 NOW
  • Tell your partner. Partner asks: Did you use to…?
  • New partner: two truths and a lie
How many topics? (One.)
Real life only? (No. A card is fine.)
Do you explain your choice? (No.)
After "did": used or use? (Use.)
