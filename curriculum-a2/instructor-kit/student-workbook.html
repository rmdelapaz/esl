📖 🗣️ New words — linking words and their jobs
  • ➕ and = add
  • ↔️ but = different
  • ➡️ so = result
  • ❓ because = reason (Why?)

Grammar box

butI like tea, but I prefer coffee.
soI was tired, so I went to bed.
becauseI went to bed because I was tired.
althoughAlthough it rained, we went out.
