Teach Elementary English to Adults, Ready to Run from Day One

Everyday English (A2 Elementary) is a complete 13-week classroom course for adults who have finished a beginner course or placed at A2: workers, parents, and newcomers who can manage the basics and now need to tell their story, make plans, and handle real situations in English. Every class is planned to the minute. It works for a trained ESL teacher and for a first-time volunteer.

Why Programs Choose It

  • Zero prep to teach. Every class adds up to 90 minutes: warmer, presentation, drill, controlled practice, speaking, and homework set-up. A volunteer can open the guide and teach.
  • Built for real adult classes. Mixed first languages, mixed literacy, and adults with busy, sometimes difficult lives. Each lesson gives literacy support and stretch options. Tasks about the past, family, and home always offer a "new identity" or an imagined story, so nobody has to share what they'd rather keep private.
  • Proven communicative method. Meaning, form, and pronunciation with concept checks, choral and individual drilling, pair work with instruction checks, and kind, delayed error correction. Good ESL practice runs through the whole course.
  • Storytelling at its heart. The past simple and past continuous, used to, the present perfect, and three ways to talk about the future, so learners can tell their own story and plan the next chapter. Two review projects pull it all together: a travel story and "My Life Story & Future Plans".
  • English for getting things done. Directions and tickets, booking a table and ordering, returning an item, and phoning a clinic about a health problem: the situations adults face every week.
  • A free companion course. Every lesson mirrors the free online course Everyday English (A2) (rays-esl.netlify.app/curriculum-a2) with 🔊 audio on every word and dialogue. It serves as homework and review, and lets learners catch up after a missed class. A free tools page shows learners how to translate lessons into their own language.
  • A clear path forward. The course is the second step of a complete free CEFR ladder (A1 → C2). It follows English Foundations (A1), and learners who finish move into Independent English (B1).
