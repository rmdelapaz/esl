Instructor-Led Course Syllabus

Business English

An 8-week, 16-class workplace-communication course for adults at B1 or above who use English at work, are looking for work, or are rebuilding a professional career in English. Twice a week for 90 minutes, you practice the real situations of working life: emails and messages, phone and video calls, meetings, networking, feedback, presentations, negotiations, pitches, reports, CVs and interviews. Between classes, the free online lessons (with audio) are your homework.

Class Schedule

Two classes a week, 90 minutes each. Twelve classes each teach one lesson from the online course, and four are assessment classes: a needs analysis in Class 1, a Midpoint Performance Check in Class 8, and a two-part Final Assessment in Classes 15 and 16. Every lesson runs as a workplace-skills workshop: a real model, the key phrases, controlled practice, a role-play or writing task, and feedback on register, tone, and clarity. Each class shows its lesson, its focus, and the ✓ can-do goal learners reach by the end.

Every Class, the Same Shape

A steady routine makes it easy to take risks in role-plays. Timings flex from lesson to lesson; the writing lessons give the writing task more time.

0:00—Warmer & review of the last classA quick task that recycles last class’s phrases, or links today’s topic to your own work
0:10—Model & key languageA real email, call, meeting, or pitch · notice the phrases, register, and tone
0:25—Controlled practiceWorkbook tasks in pairs · drilling for stress and polite intonation
0:45—Break (5 min)
0:50—Role-play or writing taskA realistic workplace simulation with role cards · or a timed document
1:10—FeedbackPeer and teacher feedback against the course rubric · delayed error correction · “I can…” checklist
1:25—Homework set-upThe online lesson with 🔊 audio + one workbook page

Course Details

Prerequisites

  • CEFR B1 or above recommended. You can handle everyday conversations, follow the main points of clear speech, and write a simple connected paragraph.
  • New to our program? Ask your teacher for the program's placement test first. Learners placing at B1 to C2 are ready for this course; mixed levels work well, because every task has support and stretch options.
  • Placed at A2 or below? Build your general English first with Independent English (B1), then join Business English. Want more grammar alongside it? Confident English (B2) pairs well with this course.
  • No job needed. Every task that asks about your work has a role-card option: a fictional job and company, so you never have to share personal details.

What’s Provided

  • A Student Workbook for all 12 lessons, with model emails, dialogues, phrase banks, role-plays, and self-checks (the learner keeps it)
  • A welcome handout (how the classes run, the course rubric in plain words, QR codes to the online lessons) and a one-page performance tracker
  • For the teacher: a Teacher’s Guide (a 90-minute plan for every class), slides, role-play cards, listening scripts, games, the needs analysis, the midpoint check, the final assessment, and answer keys
  • Free access to the companion online course: Business English, with 🔊 audio
  • A free learning-tools page that shows how to translate lessons into any language

Assessment

  • Every class: role-plays and writing tasks with feedback and an “I can…” checklist, with no grades
  • Class 1 · Needs analysis: a workplace-needs survey, a short reply email, and a 3-minute speaking sample
  • Class 8 · Midpoint Performance Check: a timed email and register rewrite, plus a phone call and a short meeting simulation
  • Classes 15–16 · Final Assessment: timed writing (a proposal section and a letter or email), a negotiation, a presentation or pitch with Q&A, and a short mock interview
  • One simple rubric throughout: Task & Purpose · Register & Tone · Language Range & Accuracy · Organization & Delivery, each scored 1–5
  • Scores are private and used to set your own targets. Everyone who completes the course receives a certificate

Homework & the Online Course

  • After each class, do the matching online lesson at home: about 45–65 minutes, with 🔊 Listen buttons for model sentences
  • Plus one workbook page, and one real-world try each week: send the email, make the call, use the phrase at work
  • Missed a class? The online lesson covers the same language, so you can catch up. Missed an assessment? Ask your teacher about a make-up slot
  • The online lessons and the classroom materials both use American English. British spellings (behaviour, organise) are also correct in international business — just stay consistent in one document

What You’ll Be Able to Do

By the end of the course, learners can handle the core communication tasks of professional life in English, clearly and with the right tone:

Choose the right register and tone for any audience, and make a strong first impression
Write clear, well-structured emails and messages for requests, updates, and bad news
Handle phone and video calls, and take accurate messages
Take an active part in meetings, and chair a short one with clear action points
Start, sustain, and end small talk, and follow up after networking
Disagree, give feedback, and say no diplomatically but firmly
Deliver a structured, well-signposted presentation and handle questions
Negotiate with conditional language and pitch an idea persuasively
Write a short report or proposal with an executive summary and clear recommendations
Present your experience in a CV, a cover letter, and a STAR-structured interview

Want to preview a lesson or catch up on a missed class? The full companion course is free online: Business English. Need more general English first? Independent English (B1) · Confident English (B2). Not sure of your level? Take the placement test (your teacher will arrange it).

All companies, people, and figures in the course materials are fictional.
