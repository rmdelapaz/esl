2.1 · Participating · the phrase banks

Reliable, ready-made phrases

FunctionPhrases
OpinionIn my view… · It seems to me that… · I'd suggest (that) we…
AgreeAbsolutely. · I'm with you on that. · That's a good point.
Disagree politelyI see your point, but… · I'm not sure I agree. · Have we considered…?
ClarifyCould you expand on that? · Just so I'm clear, are you saying…?
SuggestWhy don't we ask…? · How about asking…? · I'd suggest we ask…
"I am agree." Correct? (No: I agree.) · "discuss about the budget"? (No: discuss the budget.) · "I suggest to wait"? (No: I suggest waiting.)
2.1 · Interrupting and turn-taking · signal first

Interrupting is a skill, not a sin

Do thisSay this
come inSorry to interRUPT, but… ↗ · Can I just come IN here? ↗
build onJust to build on that… · To add to Ana's point…
hold the floorIf I could just FINish…
bring others inWhat do you think, SAra?
Are you really sorry? (Not really: it's a signal.)
What does just do? (Makes it softer.)
"If I could just finish…": who says it? (The person being interrupted.)
Flat and fast = rude. Friendly and rising = teamwork.
2.1 · Chairing · five stages

Talk least on content, most on process

StageThe chair says…
1 OpenThanks everyone for coming. Shall we get started? The purpose of today's meeting is…
2 AgendaFirst on the agenda is… · Let's move on to… · That's item one covered.
3 Time & peopleLet's hear from Tom. · We're running short on time. · Let's park that and take it offline.
4 DecideSo, to summarize… ↘ · Are we all agreed? · Can I confirm, you'll handle this by Friday?
5 CloseTo recap the action points… · Thanks everyone, that was productive.
"Let's park that." Discuss it now? (No.) Forget it? (No: later.) Offline = internet off? (No: after the meeting.)

2.2 · Lead-in and model · the coffee line

Small talk: a waste of time?

H: Are you enjoying the expo?
J: Yes, it's fine.
H: Good. It's busy.
J: Yes. (walks away)
H: This line for coffee is quite something, isn't it?
J: It really is! Everyone had the same idea.
H: What did you think of that last session?
J: Really useful. We're changing our tracking system at the moment…
Why does the first one die? (Closed questions, one-word answers, no free information.)
2.2 · The rapport engine

Ask, react, add, pass the ball back

  • Open questions: How · What · Which · Why → "What are you enjoying most so far?"
  • React: Oh REALly? ↗ · That sounds interesting, tell me more.
  • Free information: answer + extra + question back
  • Common ground: Same here, actually!
"How are you finding the conference?" Where is it? (No: your opinion so far.)
Free information: free of cost? (Extra detail you give.) Why? (It gives them a hook.)
"Great turnout, isn't it? ↘" A real question? (No: you expect agreement.)
50 / 50 listen at least as much as you talk

How many people? (At least three.)

2.3 · The softening toolkit · one or two, not five

Seasoning, not the meal

MoveExample
hedgingI think we might be underestimating the timeline.
qualifierI have a slight concern about the budget.
question formWould it be possible to push it a week? · Have we considered…?
distanceI was wondering if we could revisit the deadline.
positive framingI can definitely get you the draft by Thursday.
prefacingI take your point, but… · I see it a little differently…
"I was wondering…": about the past? (No: it's more polite now.) · "A slight concern": always small? (No: it may be big.) · "I'm at capacity": lazy? (No: no more time.)
2.3 · Feedback · give it and receive it

Situation → Behavior → Impact

✘ "Your reports are always late. Be more organized. Anyway, good job on the chart."
✔ "One thing that worked really well was the new chart. (S) In the last two months, (B) the report reached me on the morning it's due, (I) so I couldn't check the figures. How do you feel it's going?"
"You're careless." Behavior or person? (Person.)
Receive: Thanks for telling me. · Could you give me an example? · Let me take that on board.
Drill: "Your email was confusing." · "You were late to the call." · "The slides had a lot of text."
2.3 · Freer task · groups of 3 · 3 rounds × 5 min

The hard-conversation carousel

  • Say no: alternative · trade-off · buy time
  • Conflict: acknowledge the feeling → problem, not person → common ground → way forward
  • A says the hard thing · B follows the card · C observes
  • 1 min plan · 3 min talk · 1 min feedback · rotate
A: what first? (Acknowledge.)
Change the real message? (No: firm on substance.)
B: agree at once? (No: follow your card.)
How many rounds? (Three: everyone is A once.)
A real situation? (No: the card's.)
