🔊 Read Aloud

Hear any English read to you. Take a photo of a letter, a sign, a form or a page in a book — or paste some text. Then listen, slowly, one sentence at a time.

1 Get the words

You can also drag a picture here, or paste a screenshot (Ctrl+V / ⌘+V). 🔒 Your picture stays on your device. It is never uploaded.

2 Listen

🔇 No English voice was found in this browser. Scanning still works, but nothing can be read aloud here. This works on most phones and on Windows, Mac and Chromebook. Try another browser (Chrome, Edge or Safari), or add an English voice in your device's language settings, then reload.

👆 How to use it

  • ▶ Read it to me reads everything. The word you hear is colored.
  • Tap a word in the gray box to hear only that word.
  • Tap the small ▶ before a sentence to hear only that sentence. Tap it again and again — that's OK!
  • Too fast? Move Speed to the left.
  • Listen & repeat waits after each sentence. Say the sentence out loud, then it goes on.
  • 🌐 Translate opens Google Translate with your words (so they are sent to Google). To translate only a part, select it first.

📷 Tips for a good photo

  • Put the paper flat on a table. Hold the phone straight above it.
  • Use good light. No shadows on the paper.
  • Get close. The words should fill the photo.
  • Printed words work best. Handwriting is hard to read.
  • Always check the words in the box. You can fix mistakes before you listen.

💡 Ideas: what can I read?

  • Letters from your child's school, or from the doctor or the bank
  • Forms — a job application, a rental form
  • Labels — food, medicine, cleaning products
  • Signs and notices at work or in your building
  • A page from a book or a lesson you are studying

⚠️ For medicine and important papers, ask a pharmacist, doctor or another person to check too. The scanner can make mistakes.
