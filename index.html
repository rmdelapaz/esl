🌟 Structured Curriculum

  • English Foundations (A1 Beginner)
    A complete, self-paced beginner course — 24 real lessons from the alphabet to your first conversations. Start here if you're learning English step by step.
  • Everyday English (A2 Elementary)
    The next step after A1 — 24 lessons on the past tense, comparing things, the future, and life experiences, so you can tell stories and handle everyday situations.
  • Independent English (B1 Intermediate)
    The intermediate level — 24 lessons on perfect tenses, conditionals, reported speech, relative clauses, the passive, and giving opinions. Become an independent speaker.
  • Confident English (B2 Upper-Intermediate)
    24 lessons on narrative tenses, hypotheticals, verb patterns, advanced passives, participle clauses, discourse markers, and emphasis. Reach real fluency.
  • Advanced English (C1 Advanced)
    24 lessons on advanced aspect & modality, the subjunctive, inversion, nominalisation, cohesion, hedging, phrasal verbs, and collocation. Command register and style.
  • Mastery English (C2 Proficiency)
    The summit — 24 lessons on the mot juste, register agility, idiom, implicature, irony, figurative language, discourse mastery, rhetoric, and personal style. Near-native artistry.

🎯 Exam Preparation

  • IELTS Academic Preparation
    A complete 12-lesson course for the IELTS Academic test — all four skills, every question type, the band descriptors, model answers, and test-day strategy. Best from B1–B2 and up.
  • Business English
    A 12-lesson professional-communication course — emails, calls, meetings, small talk, presentations, negotiations, business writing, and job interviews. Practical workplace English from B1 up.
