Teach Mastery English: The Top of the Ladder, Ready to Run

Mastery English (C2 Proficiency) is a complete 13-week classroom course for adults who have completed an advanced course or placed at C2: translators and interpreters, teachers of English, academics and graduate students, senior professionals, and candidates for proficiency exams. Their English is already fluent and accurate; this course makes it exact, nuanced, and effective. Every class is planned to the minute and built around a demanding workshop task: close reading, editing, rhetorical analysis, stylistic writing, negotiation, or public speaking. It works for an experienced ESL teacher and for a well-prepared volunteer with a strong command of English.

Why Programs Choose It

  • Mastery, not more grammar. C2 learners rarely need another rule. They need the mot juste, register agility, idiom and restricted collocation, implicature, irony, figurative language, stance, cohesion across long texts, rhetoric, and a voice of their own, each taught as a set of choices and practiced for effect.
  • Workshops that work. Every lesson ends in a demanding task (a close reading, an editing pass, a persuasive speech, a negotiation, a critique) run with a clear protocol, so feedback targets precision, appropriacy, naturalness, and effect, not just accuracy.
  • Exam preparation without teaching to the test. A dedicated lesson on proficiency-exam technique (in the style of Cambridge C2 Proficiency and IELTS at band 8 and above), and a final test with original items in those formats, give candidates a realistic sense of the task types while the rest of the course builds the underlying command. All items are original; the kit is independent of every exam board.
  • Built for professional, academic, and translation users. Writing for a real readership (op-eds, reviews, long-form reports, abstracts), fine points of usage and punctuation, cross-cultural pragmatics, diplomacy and negotiation, translation and interpreting awareness, and public speaking: the work that C2 users actually do.
  • World Englishes, respectfully. Learners study British, American, and global varieties as equals, and learn to understand fast, authentic, reference-heavy media. No learner is ever asked to perform an accent or to speak "for" a culture.
  • Debate without division. Topics stay in workplace, education, science, arts and culture, technology, language, and community territory, never party politics or religion. Every argument task offers an assigned side; humor is studied without ever targeting a nationality, a religion, an accent, or a disability.
  • A free companion course. Every lesson mirrors the free online course Mastery English (C2) (rays-esl.netlify.app/curriculum-c2) with 🔊 audio on model sentences and dialogues. It serves as homework and review and lets learners catch up after a missed class.
  • A clear finish, and what comes after. The course completes a free CEFR ladder (A1 → C2) and follows Advanced English (C1). Its final lesson turns learners toward lifelong command, and the assessment key maps each learner's result to a concrete next step.
