7.1 · Meaning · the directness dial

Same act, different dial

ActMore directMore indirect
RequestSend me the file today.I don't suppose there's any chance of the file today?
RefusalNo, that won't work for me.That would be quite challenging…
CriticismThis section is wrong.I wonder whether this section might read a little differently?
DisagreementI don't agree.I see it slightly differently, though I may be missing something.
KEY Neither end is "more polite." And an indirect refusal is still a refusal: read the softener as content.

7.2 · Form · the engine of negotiation · and face

If you were able to X, then we could Y

If you were able to commit to a year, we could hold the rate.
We could move on price, provided the timeline shifts.
What if we were to add Saturdays?
Shall we say three percent, twelve months?
Save their face:
The timing has been against us.
Your logic was sound given what we knew.
With the new figures in, it'd be completely reasonable to revisit this.
How do we solve this together?
GRAMMAR provided you will sign sign · as long as you pay · on condition that you commit
7.2 · Model · where are the moves?

A delicate negotiation

Rosa: I'll be straight with you: I need to raise something difficult about the pricing.

Daniel: Go on. Although the budget on our side is more or less locked.

Rosa: I understand, and I appreciate you saying so. Costs have risen across the whole sector.

Rosa: If you were able to commit to twelve months, then we could hold the rate much closer to where it is.

Daniel: A longer term I can defend: it looks like stability, not a cost.

Rosa: Shall we say three percent, twelve months, rate frozen? I'll put it in writing today.

Daniel's real interest? (Predictability; a story he can sell upstairs.) Who lost? (Nobody.)

7.3 · The untranslatable · the paraphrase pattern

Signal → image → meaning → feeling

Ana: There's a word in my language, sobremesa. Do you have it in English?

Tom: I don't think we have a single word for that. What does it mean, literally?

Ana: Literally it's "over-the-table." Roughly, it's the time you spend still sitting and talking after a meal.

Tom: The closest we'd get is "chatting over coffee after dinner," but that loses the sense that it's a whole custom.

hygge · saudade · Schadenfreude · tsundoku · nunchi · Feierabend. Untranslatable? (Not one-word-able. Conveyable.)
7.3 · Between speakers · and the ethics

Translation, interpreting & the interpreter's code

Translationwritten · time to craft
Consecutivespeaker pauses · interpreter renders the chunk
Simultaneousalmost at once · usually a booth
Sight translationa written text read aloud in another language
Accurate & complete: soften a rude remark? (No.)
Impartial: give advice? (No.)
Confidential · transparent: missed a number? ("Interpreter speaking: could you repeat that?")
First person: "I have two children."
NOTE Register and tone must survive the crossing. High-stakes settings need professional interpreters, not children.
