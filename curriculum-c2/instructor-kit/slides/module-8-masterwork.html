8.1 · Use of English · the signature task

Key word transformation: the four checks

NO · 3–6 wordsI couldn't understand the lecture at all. → I could make no sense of the lecture.
SOONERAs soon as she arrived, the argument started. → No sooner had she arrived than the argument started.
The checks① key word unchanged · ② word limit (a contraction = 2) · ③ same meaning · ④ fits both sides of the gap
"No sooner she had arrived…" Mark? (Zero: no inversion.) SOONER → "soon"? (Zero: key word changed.) "I'll" counts as? (Two words.)

8.1 · Reading & listening · evidence, not instinct

Follow the breadcrumbs

Gapped textThe missing sentence must fit what comes before AND what comes after.
Breadcrumbsthis compression · such answers · the latter · However, · by contrast
ListeningThe first thing mentioned is often the trap: listen for what the speaker settles on.
The next sentence begins "This compression…". What must the gap mention? (Something compressed.)
True in the world, not in the text? (Wrong.)
"I thought it was the budget, but actually it came down to timing." Answer? (Timing.)
8.1 · Writing · summarize AND evaluate

The essay summary

The taskTwo short texts, one question. Summarize and evaluate the key points of both, and give your own view, in your own words.
¶1The shared question; the two positions
¶2 · ¶3Each text: key points paraphrased + one strength, one weakness
¶4Your view, weighed: where they meet, where you stand
Only summarize? (Under-answered.) Copy the best sentence? (No: paraphrase shows range.) "Nowadays, with the development of society…"? (Formulaic: engage the texts.)

8.2 · Class C24 · the stage

Same words. Two deliveries.

An audience forgets most of what you say and remembers how you made them feel. A listener cannot rewind.

Were the words different? (No.) Which version do you remember? Why? (Pauses, stress, a slower close.) Is delivery vanity? (No: it makes meaning easy to receive.)

Open · spine · close

SpineI want to leave you with three thoughts, and here's the first.
CloseSo the next time you can't remember making a choice: pause.

Antithesisknown more | understood less
AnaphoraNot in a year. Not in a decade.

Seven words, five meanings

I never said she stole the money.a flat denial
I never said she stole the money.I only implied it

Stress every word? (Robotic: one word per idea.) Stress "the"? (Almost never.)
8.2 · The pause · one idea, one breath

Silence on purpose

Line 1 Every expert you admire || was once catastrophically bad | at the thing they're now famous for. ||

Line 2 Every single one. ||

Line 3 The gap between you and the person you admire | is not talent. || It is time.

| short pause · || long pause. Before a key line? ("Listen: this matters.") After? ("Let it land.") Two seconds feels long to whom? (The speaker, not the audience.)

8.3 · Model · a masterwork and its discussion

Concede, then narrow

Speaker: Fluency is the door. What lies beyond it… that is the room. And I spent years, frankly, mistaking the door for the room.

Discussant: Isn't there something a little elitist in it?

Speaker: That's a genuinely important challenge, and you're right to press it. Let me draw the distinction I actually mean. I'm not saying mastery makes a person richer; I'm saying it opens particular rooms.

Does she reject the challenge? (No.) Give up her position? (No.) What does she do? (Concedes the risk, then narrows the claim.) Weaker or stronger now? (Smaller, and stronger.)
