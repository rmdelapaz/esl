Mastery English (C2 Proficiency)

A 13-week, 26-class course for adults who already command English and now want mastery: the exact word, the right register, the meaning beneath the words, and prose and speech that move people. Twice a week for 90 minutes, you work on nuance and idiom, irony and imagery, stance and cohesion, rhetoric and style, world Englishes and literature, writing for real readers, diplomacy and mediation between languages, exam technique and public speaking, and you finish by delivering and defending a masterwork. Between classes, the free online lessons (with audio) are your homework.

Class Schedule

Two classes a week, 90 minutes each. Every class has one lesson from the online course, except the two assessment classes, and every class ends in a demanding workshop: close reading, editing, stylistic writing, negotiation, or a talk. Lessons 4.3 and 8.3 are review projects: a halfway masterwork, and a final masterwork delivered and defended in discussion. Each class shows its lesson, its focus, and the ✓ can-do goal learners reach by the end.

Course Details

Prerequisites

  • CEFR C1. You can place events precisely in time and calibrate certainty, use inversion, clefts, and nominal style, write essays, reports, and professional emails, read arguments critically, follow lectures, and hold your own in a seminar.
  • Finished Advanced English (C1)? You’re ready. New to our program? Take the shared placement test first.
  • Learners whose reading and writing lag behind their speaking are welcome. Every lesson has support options, and the tests have an adapted version.
  • No computer needed in class. A phone or computer helps for homework and for recording yourself, but it isn’t required.

What’s Provided

  • A Student Workbook for all 24 lessons, written in C2 English (the learner keeps it)
  • A welcome handout with workshop, critique, and discussion norms, and QR codes to the online lessons
  • For the teacher: a Teacher’s Guide (a 90-minute plan for every class), slides, role-play and negotiation cards, listening scripts, games, and answer keys
  • Free access to the companion online course: Mastery English (C2), with 🔊 audio
  • A free learning-tools page that shows how to translate lessons into any language

Assessment

  • Every class: an “I can…” checklist and critique of the workshop task, with no grades
  • Class 13 · Progress Test 1 (Modules 1–4): listening for implicature and attitude, word choice and collocation, register rewrites, stance, a close reading of a column, the opening of a persuasive talk, and a 9-minute pair speaking task
  • Class 26 · Final Test (all 8 modules): listening, a literary close reading, original proficiency-style Use of English, an op-ed, review, or synthesis (about 300 words), and a 12-minute individual interview with a long turn, an abstract discussion under push-back, and a negotiation or diplomatic task
  • Rubrics follow the CEFR C2 descriptors. Results point to the next step, never to who gets to celebrate
  • Tasks about your life can always use a real, general, or imagined example, and in debates you may argue an assigned side instead of your own view

Homework & the Online Course

  • After each class, do the matching online lesson at home: about 45–60 minutes, with 🔊 Listen buttons for model sentences and dialogues
  • Plus one workbook page, and serious reading in English every day
  • Missed a class? The online lesson covers the same ground, so you can catch up
  • After the course: C2 is the top of the CEFR ladder. A proficiency exam is an option if you need the credential; IELTS Academic Preparation and Business English are free specialist tracks

What You’ll Be Able to Do

By the end of the course, learners work at CEFR C2: they understand virtually everything they hear or read and express themselves spontaneously, very fluently, and precisely, differentiating finer shades of meaning even in complex situations:

Choose the exactly right word for connotation, force, register, and company
Shift a message across the whole register cline, and control tone independently
Use idiom, binomials, and restricted collocation exactly and sparingly
Decode and deliver implicature, irony, understatement, and figurative meaning
Signal stance precisely and build long texts that cohere
Summarize, paraphrase, and synthesize sources faithfully and fairly
Persuade with the classical appeals and figures, and detect manipulation
Follow world Englishes, literature, and fast authentic media with ease
Write op-eds, reviews, and long-form pieces to an educated reader's standard
Negotiate, mediate between languages, and deliver a talk with impact
