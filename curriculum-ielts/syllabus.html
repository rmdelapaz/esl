IELTS Academic Preparation

An 8-week, 16-class exam-preparation course for adults at B2 or above who need an IELTS Academic band score for university entry, professional registration, or a visa. Twice a week for 90 minutes, you learn how every section of the test works, practice each major question type, write and self-mark both Writing tasks, rehearse the Speaking interview, and sit a diagnostic and two full practice tests. Between classes, the free online lessons (with audio) are your homework.

Level

Class Schedule

Two classes a week, 90 minutes each. Twelve classes each teach one lesson from the online course, and four are assessment classes: a diagnostic in Class 1, Practice Test 1 (Listening and Reading) in Class 8, and Practice Test 2 across Classes 15 and 16. Every lesson runs as an exam-skills workshop: strategy, guided practice, timed practice, self-marking against paraphrased band criteria, and targeted feedback. Each class shows its lesson, its focus, and the ✓ can-do goal learners reach by the end.

Course Details

Prerequisites

  • CEFR B2 or above recommended. You can follow fast natural speech on familiar topics, read longer articles, argue a point, and write a structured paragraph without much help.
  • New to our program? Ask your teacher for the program's placement test first. Learners placing at B2, C1, or C2 are ready for this course.
  • Placed at B1 or below, or at B2 with a large gap to your target band? Build your general English first with Confident English (B2) or Advanced English (C1). Exam technique works best on a solid base.
  • No computer needed in class. A phone or computer helps for homework and for recording speaking practice, but it isn’t required.

What’s Provided

  • A Student Workbook for all 12 lessons, with original passages, charts, prompts, and cue cards (the learner keeps it)
  • A welcome handout (timed-practice etiquette, test-anxiety tips, honest band expectations, QR codes to the online lessons) and a one-page band tracker
  • For the teacher: a Teacher’s Guide (a 90-minute plan for every class), slides, listening scripts, cue cards, games, the diagnostic, two practice tests, and answer keys
  • Free access to the companion online course: IELTS Academic Preparation, with 🔊 audio
  • A free learning-tools page that shows how to translate lessons into any language

Assessment

  • Every class: timed practice, self-marking, and an “I can…” checklist, with no grades
  • Class 1 · Diagnostic: short Listening and Reading sections, a writing sample, and a 3-minute speaking sample
  • Class 8 · Practice Test 1: timed Listening and Reading, self-marked and converted to bands in Class 9
  • Classes 15–16 · Practice Test 2: timed Listening and Reading, then Writing Task 1 + Task 2 (60 min) and a short individual speaking interview, followed by a band debrief
  • Writing and Speaking are assessed against criteria paraphrased from the public IELTS band descriptors. All bands are estimates for planning, not official scores
  • Scores are private. A gain of about half a band in eight weeks is common; bigger jumps are possible but not guaranteed

Homework & the Online Course

  • After each class, do the matching online lesson at home: about 45–60 minutes, with 🔊 Listen buttons for model sentences
  • Plus one workbook page, 15 minutes of English every day, and one timed task each week from your band tracker plan
  • Missed a class? The online lesson covers the same strategies, so you can catch up. Missed a practice test? Ask your teacher how to sit it at home, timed
  • The online lessons use British spelling; the classroom materials use American English. IELTS accepts both, used consistently

What You’ll Be Able to Do

By the end of the course, learners know the IELTS Academic test inside out and can perform closer to their true level under exam conditions:

Explain the format, timing, and scoring, and set a realistic target with per-skill minimums
Recognize paraphrase in questions and use it to raise Lexical Resource
Use a range of complex structures while keeping errors under control
Predict Listening answers, avoid distractors, and meet spelling and word-limit rules
Skim, scan, and manage 60 minutes across three Reading passages
Tell False from Not Given and match headings with a repeatable method
Write a Task 1 report with a clear overview in about 20 minutes
Plan and write a Task 2 essay that fully answers the question in about 40 minutes
Extend Speaking answers and deliver a two-minute Part 2 long turn
Self-assess Writing and Speaking against the criteria, manage nerves, and plan the final weeks to test day
