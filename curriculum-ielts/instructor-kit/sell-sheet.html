Teach IELTS Academic Preparation: the Score for University Entry and Professional Registration

IELTS Academic Preparation is a complete 8-week classroom course for adults at B2 or above who need a band score to start a degree, register in their profession (nursing, medicine, engineering, teaching), or meet a visa requirement. Every class is an exam-skills workshop planned to the minute: strategy, guided practice, timed practice, self-marking against paraphrased band criteria, and targeted feedback. It works for a trained ESL teacher and for a well-prepared volunteer, with no previous IELTS examining experience required.

Why Programs Choose It

  • Zero prep to teach. Every class adds up to 90 minutes: a warmer, a strategy input, guided practice, a timed task, self-marking, feedback, and homework set-up. A teacher can open the guide and teach, even if they have never taught IELTS before.
  • Built around the real stakes. Learners need a specific band for a specific institution: a university, a nursing or medical board, an engineering or teaching regulator. The course starts by finding each learner's exact requirement, including per-skill minimums, and every class works toward it.
  • Every section, every major question type. Listening (predicting, distractors, spelling and word limits), Reading (skimming, scanning, True/False/Not Given, matching headings), Writing Task 1 (overviews and trend language) and Task 2 (question types, planning, argument), and Speaking Parts 1–3, plus paraphrasing and grammar for a higher band.
  • Assessment that teaches. A diagnostic and two full practice tests, spaced so learners can act on the results. Learners self-mark against paraphrased criteria, tally their error types on a band tracker, and plan each week around the evidence.
  • Honest and kind about scores. The materials set realistic expectations (a half-band gain in eight weeks is common; bigger jumps are not guaranteed), address test anxiety directly, and give teachers the language to advise on test dates without discouraging anyone.
  • 100% original test material. Every reading passage, listening script, chart, writing prompt, and speaking question was written for this course. Nothing is copied from published test books, so you can print and use it freely under your license. Topics stay neutral: education, science and the environment, technology, health habits, urban life, work, and culture.
  • A free companion course. Every lesson mirrors the free online course IELTS Academic Preparation (rays-esl.netlify.app/curriculum-ielts) with annotated model answers, exercises, and 🔊 audio on model sentences. It serves as homework and review, and lets learners catch up after a missed class.
  • Part of a complete ladder. The course runs alongside a free CEFR ladder (A1 → C2). Learners who are not yet at B2 can build their general English first with Confident English (B2) or Advanced English (C1), and come back to exam preparation when they are ready.
