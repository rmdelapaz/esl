Overall band = the average of the four skills, rounded to the nearest half band (an average ending in .25 rounds up to the next half band; .75 rounds up to the next whole band). Example: 6.5 + 6.0 + 6.0 + 7.0 = 25.5 ÷ 4 = 6.375 → 6.5. Also check each skill against any minimum per skill your institution sets. Your teacher converts raw scores to bands with the tables in the answer key.
