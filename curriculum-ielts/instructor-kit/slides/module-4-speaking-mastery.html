Grow the answer

✘ "Yes, I do." (What can the examiner mark?)
AnswerYes, I really do, especially on weekends,
Whybecause it helps me switch off after work.
ExampleLast Sunday, for instance, I made soup for my whole building.

4.1 · Part 2 · the cue card

One minute of keywords. Two minutes of talk.

Describe a piece of technology you find useful in your daily life.
You should say:
· what it is
· how long you have had it
· how you use it
and explain why it is so useful to you.
My notes: rice cooker + timer · 3 yrs, longer hours · rinse → timer 6:00 → hot rice · porridge (friend!) · saves time + $ · no takeout · reliable → "biggest difference"
Sentences or keywords? (Keywords.) Stop at 1:20 if no one speaks? (No: keep going until "Thank you.") Out of ideas? (A reason, an example, a comparison, a feeling.)
4.1 · Part 3 · a small argument

Opinion → reason → other view → resolution → future

Examiner Do you think technology makes life simpler or more complicated?
You I'd argue that it makes everyday tasks simpler. Paying bills used to take real effort; now it takes seconds. That said, we're expected to be available all the time. So perhaps it simplifies tasks but complicates our time. In the long run, I imagine people will get better at setting limits.
Is the examiner asking about your phone? (No: people in general.) Must you have a strong opinion? (No: "It depends on…" is fine, if you explain.)
HEDGE I tend to think… · It's likely that… · I suppose… · I suspect…

A band is a quote, not a feeling

Tomás Really? It felt like I was rambling.
Mina Lexical Resource is more like a 6. You said "nice" three times, but "a heavy workload" was great.
Tomás Fair enough. And pronunciation?

4.3 · Nerves and results

Calm, prepared, on strategy

  • Breathe: in for 4, out for 6, three times
  • Self-talk: "One mark each: guess, mark it, move on."
  • If–then: If I miss an answer, I'll go to the next one.
  • Routine: normal breakfast · arrive early · no cramming
After the test: results online first, then the Test Report Form. Think a score is wrong? Ask your test center for a remark within the allowed period (a fee, refunded if your score goes up). Retake the full test, or in many places one skill. Check that your university or employer accepts it.
