4.1

The Passive — How It's Made

Class C10
📖 🗣️ New words — products and processes
  • coffee: the beans are grown, picked and roasted
  • tea: the leaves are picked by hand
  • rice: it is grown in wet fields
  • chocolate: it is made from cacao beans
  • cheese: it is made from milk
  • bread: it is baked in an oven
  • bananas: they are picked green
  • sugar: it is made from sugar cane

⚙️ Process verbs (verb → past participle):

grow → grownpick → pickeddry → driedroast → roastedgrind → groundpack → packedsell → soldexport → exported

🏛️ History verbs:

build → builtinvent → inventeddesign → designeddiscover → discoveredwrite → writtenfound → founded

made of wood (you can see it) · made from milk (it changed) · made in Italy (the place) · made by hand / my sister (who)

Grammar box

The passive = be + past participle. Only be changes.

Now / alwaysam / is / are + participleEnglish is spoken here. · These cars are made in Japan.
Pastwas / were + participleThe bridge was built in 1890. · The letters were sent yesterday.
❌ Noisn't / aren't / wasn't / weren'tPlastic isn't recycled here. · The package wasn't delivered.
❓ Question(Wh-) is / are / was / were + subject + participle?Is this made with milk? · When was it built?
Why use it?We don't know who did it. · It isn't important. · It's obvious. · It's "people in general." · We want to talk about a process.
by + who?Only when the doer is interesting: Hamlet was written by Shakespeare. Don't say "by someone" or "by people."

📖 🗣️ Dialogue — read with a partner

Tina: Mmm, this coffee is really good. Where is it from?

Leo: It's from Colombia. It's grown in the mountains, and the beans are picked by hand.

Tina: By hand? That's a lot of work! Is it roasted there too?

Leo: No, it's roasted here, in a small shop downtown. It was opened last year.

Tina: And this mug is beautiful. Was it made in Mexico?

Leo: No, it was made by my daughter! In her pottery class.

Tina: Wow. Tell her it's the best mug I've ever used! ☕

4.2

Must, Might, Can't — Detectives at Work

Class C11
📖 🗣️ New words — clues and deductions
  • a wet umbrella → It must be raining.
  • a lot of luggage → She might be going on a trip.
  • a passport in the bag → It can't be a child's bag.
  • keys on the table → He must be home.
  • gloves and a hat → It could be cold outside.
  • no answer → Her phone might be off.
a clueevidencea deduction (a guess with a reason)sure / certainpossibleimpossiblea rulenecessaryoptional (your choice)not allowedadvice
Grammar box

How sure are you? modal + base verb (no to, no -s)

✅ Sure YESmustThe light's on, so she must be home.
🤔 Maybemight / may / couldShe's not answering. She might be busy.
❌ Sure NOcan't (not "mustn't"!)He can't be hungry. He just ate.

must has two jobs: 📜 a rule: "You must sign here." · 🕵️ a deduction: "You must be tired."

📜 Necessaryhave to / mustYou have to show ID at the bank.
🆓 Not necessarydon't have toIt's free. You don't have to pay.
🚫 Not allowedmustn't (US: can't)You mustn't park here.
💡 Adviceshould / shouldn'tYou look tired. You should rest.

📖 🗣️ Dialogue — read with a partner

A: Someone left a bag on the train. Whose do you think it is?

B: Well, there's a laptop and some business cards inside, so it can't be a child's.

A: True. And there's a train ticket to Chicago. The owner might be going there for work.

B: Look, the coffee's still warm. They must be somewhere on the train right now!

A: Good thinking. They could be in the next car. We should tell the conductor.

B: Yes. We have to take it to the conductor. We mustn't just leave it here.

Review Project — Opinions & Storytelling

Class C12
📖 🗣️ New phrases — opinions

💬 Give an opinion

In my opinion, …I think / believe / feel (that) …If you ask me, …As far as I'm concerned, …It seems to me that …Personally, I'd say …

🧱 Support it (a reason + an example)

The main reason is (that) …For example, …On the one hand … on the other hand …What's more, …That's because …

👍 Agree

That's a good point.Exactly!I couldn't agree more.You're absolutely right.So do I. / Neither do I.

🙏 Disagree politely: first show you listened, then give your idea.

I see what you mean, but …That may be true, but …I'm not sure I agree.I take your point, but …I'd look at it differently.

A good opinion = claim (what I think) + reason (why) + example.

📖 🗣️ Dialogue — read with a partner

Ana: If you ask me, working from home is better than working in an office.

Ben: Really? What's the main reason?

Ana: You don't have to travel. For example, my brother used to spend two hours a day on the bus.

Ben: That's a good point. But I see it differently. If I worked at home, I'd feel lonely.

Ana: I see what you mean. You can't have lunch with your team on a screen!

Ben: Exactly. It seems to me that two days at home and three in the office is best.

Ana: I couldn't agree more. 🙂
