If & Imagining (Conditionals)

Talk about what is always true, what will really happen, and what you only imagine. Share life hacks, discuss "What would you do if…?", give friendly advice, and make small wishes. One big idea: the verb shows how real the situation is.

The reality line

Always true → If you heat ice, it melts. · Real, likely → If it rains, I'll take the bus. · Imagined → If I won the lottery, I'd travel. · Wished for → I wish it were sunny.

2.1

Zero & First Conditional — Life Hacks, Superstitions & Real Plans

Class C4
📖 🗣️ New words — life hacks and superstitions

A life hack 🔧 = a clever, simple trick for everyday life. A superstition 🍀 = an old belief about good luck or bad luck.

  • boil (water boils at 212°F)
  • freeze / melt
  • go hard / get soft
  • charge (a phone)
  • open an umbrella indoors
  • break a mirror
  • spill salt
  • my hand itches
  • miss the bus
  • can't sleep
good luck / bad luckbelieve (in)a warningalways truelikelywhen (I'm sure)if (maybe)unless (= if … not)
Grammar box
🔧 ZeroIf / When + present, presentIf you heat ice, it melts. · When I'm tired, I drink coffee.
📅 FirstIf + present, will / won't + verbIf it rains this afternoon, I'll take the bus. · If we leave now, we won't be late.
➕ Resultcan / might + verb, or an orderIf you finish early, you can go home. · If you see John, tell him hi.

🔧 Zero = always true (a fact, a habit, a life hack). 📅 First = one real, likely future.

⛔ Never put will after if or when. If it will rain rains, I'll stay home. · When I will get get home, I'll call you.

whenI'm sure it will happen.When I get home, I'll call you.
ifMaybe it will happen.If I get the job, I'll celebrate.
unless= if … not (+ a positive verb)I'll be late unless I leave now.

✏️ Comma: If first → comma: If I see her, I'll tell her. · If second → no comma: I'll tell her if I see her.

2.2

Second Conditional — Dreams, Dilemmas & Advice

Class C5
📖 🗣️ New words — dreams and dilemmas

A dilemma 🤔 = a difficult choice. There is no one right answer.

  • 💰 win the lottery
  • 👛 find a wallet
  • 🦸 have a superpower
  • 🫥 be invisible
  • travel the world
  • be a famous singer
  • open a restaurant
  • a cashier / give change
  • 🏖️ a house by the sea
  • 💼 quit my job
give it backkeep ithonestgive adviceimaginaryunlikelyimpossible
Grammar box
✅ YesIf + past simple, would ('d) + verbIf I won the lottery, I'd buy a house by the sea.
❌ Nodidn't + verb · wouldn't + verbIf I didn't work so much, I wouldn't be so tired.
❓ QuestionWhat would you do if…?What would you do if you found a wallet?
🧍 bewere for everyoneIf I were rich… · If it were warmer…
➕ Resultcould / might + verbIf I had a car, I could drive you home.

💭 The past verb (won, had, found) is not past time. It means: this is not real. "If I had a car…" = I don't have a car.

📅 First (real)If I have time, I'll call you.Maybe I'll have time.
💭 Second (dream)If I had time, I'd call you.But I don't have time.

💬 Advice: If I were you, I'd… · If I were you, I wouldn't… (friendly, softer than "You should…")

Wish & If Only — Small Wishes, Real Fixes

Class C6
📖 🗣️ New words — small, everyday wishes

☀️ The weather

  • sunny
  • rainy / gray
  • hot
  • cold

🎸 Skills

  • sing
  • swim
  • dance
  • play the guitar
  • cook

🚌 The commute (the trip to work or school)

  • wait at the bus stop
  • a crowded train
  • an expensive ticket
  • 🚗 traffic
  • ⏰ on time / late
I wish…If only…!I hope…annoying 😤a fix (a solution)
Grammar box
🌠 wish + pastI want the present to be different.I wish I lived closer to work. · I wish I didn't have so much homework.
🧍 wish + werewere for everyoneI wish it were sunny. · I wish I were taller.
🎸 wish + coulda skill I don't haveI wish I could sing.
😤 wish + wouldsomeone or something else annoys meI wish the bus would come on time.
❗ If only…a stronger wishIf only it were Friday!

🔄 The flip: a wish is the opposite of reality. I wish I had a car = I don't have a car.

⛔ Not about yourself: I wish I would be were more organized.

🤞 hopeIt's possible.I hope it's sunny tomorrow.
🌠 wishIt's not real now.I wish it were sunny (but it's raining).
