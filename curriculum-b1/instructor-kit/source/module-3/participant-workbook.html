3.1

What Did They Say? — Reported Statements

Class C7
📖 🗣️ New words — passing on news
  • the news
  • a text / a voicemail
  • an email

🗣️ Useful phrases for passing on news:

She said (that)…He told me (that)…Guess what!You'll never guess…I heard that…Apparently,…The news said…

direct speech = the exact words, in "quotation marks" · reported speech = the words in your sentence, later

Grammar box

After said or told, move the verb one step back (the "backshift"):

"I am tired."She said she was tired.
"I work hard."He said he worked hard.
"I'm working."He said he was working.
"I've finished."She said she had finished.
"I saw her."He said he had seen her.
"I'll help."She said she would help.
"I can swim."He said he could swim.
"I may come."She said she might come.
"I must go."He said he had to go.

No change: would, could, might, should, and the past perfect (had + past participle).

PronounsI → he / she · we → they · my → his / her · you (me) → I
Time & placenow → then · today → that day · tomorrow → the next day · yesterday → the day before · here → there · this → that · ago → before

Change time and place words only if the time or place is really different now.

SAYShe said (that) she was busy.no person after said
TELLShe told me (that) she was busy.tell + a person · no to

📖 🗣️ Dialogue — read with a partner

A: Guess what! I saw Sam yesterday.

B: Really? How is he?

A: Great. He told me he was moving to Chicago in June. He said he'd found a great apartment near the lake.

B: Wow! Did he say anything about work?

A: Yes. He said he had a new job at a bank, and he would start on June 1st.

B: I'll miss him!

A: Me too. But he said we could visit any time. He told me his sister lived there too, and she would show us around.

B: That's so nice. I'll call him tonight.

3.2

What Did They Ask? What Did They Want? — Questions & Commands

Class C8
📖 🗣️ New words — people who give us messages
  • the doctor
  • the nurse
  • my child's teacher
  • the mechanic
  • 💼 my boss / my manager
  • 🏢 the building manager
  • 💊 the pharmacist
  • 🗂️ the receptionist

🗣️ Reporting verbs:

askedwanted to knowtold (me) toasked (me) toreminded (me) towarned (me) not toadvised (me) to
Grammar box

A reported question is not a question any more: subject + verb, no do / does / did, no "?".

Wh- question"Where do you live?"She asked where I lived.
"What are you doing?"He asked what I was doing.
Yes/No question"Are you ready?"He asked if I was ready.
"Do you speak French?"She asked whether I spoke French.

if = whether here. Whether sounds like weather.

Command"Sit down!"She told me to sit down.
Request"Please help me."He asked me to help him.
Negative"Don't worry."She told me not to worry.

tell = an order or instruction · ask = a polite request · Always a person after them: told me, asked us.

The Friend Who…, the Place Where… — Relative Clauses

Class C9
📖 🗣️ New words — people, places and things to define
  • a nurse
  • a chef
  • a mechanic
  • a server / waiter
  • an airport
  • a post office
  • a passport
  • a fridge

🗣️ Sentence starters:

It's a person who…It's a thing which…It's a place where…It's the time when…someone whose……which is why…
Grammar box
👤 whopeopleThe teacher who helped me was very kind.
📦 whichthings, animalsThe phone which I bought is broken.
👤📦 thatpeople or things (no commas only)The book that won the prize is here.
📍 whereplacesThis is the café where we met.
🕐 whentimesI remember the day when we moved.
🔑 whosehis / her / theirThat's the man whose dog barks all night.
Defining: WHICH one?Non-defining: an extra fact
ExampleThe man who lives next door is a doctor.My brother, who lives in Chicago, is a nurse.
Commas?NoYes, two
that?Yes, OKNo. Only who / which
Drop the pronoun?Only if it is the object: the movie (that) I sawNever

✂️ The movie (that) I watched was great. ✅ (a new subject, I, comes next) · The man who called me was angry. (keep who!)
