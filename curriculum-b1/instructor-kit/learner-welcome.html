Welcome to Independent English! 👋

Welcome, and thank you for joining us. You can already handle a lot of everyday English. In this course you will learn to tell stories in detail, give and defend your opinions, report what people said, and deal with real situations at work and in daily life: discussions, complaints, and job interviews.

Some of you finished our A2 class, and some of you are new. Everyone brings something to this class, and everyone is welcome.

🤝 How our class works

  • 🕐Please arrive on time. Every class starts with a speaking warm-up.
  • 📵Keep your phone on silent. We use phones only for class activities.
  • 🗣️Speak English as much as you can. Mistakes are part of learning, so don't wait until your sentence is perfect.
  • 🙋Not sure what something means? Ask. Your question probably helps someone else too.
  • 📩If you can't come, please let your teacher know, and do the online lesson at home.
  • 🔒Your personal story is yours. When we talk about our lives, you can always use a real or an imagined story.

💬 How we discuss

At B1 we have a lot of discussions. Different opinions make a class interesting, so here's how we keep them friendly:

  • 👂Listen to the end. Let people finish before you answer.
  • ⚖️Share the time. If you've spoken a lot, invite someone else: "What do you think?"
  • 💡Give a reason for your opinion. "I think…, because…"
  • 💛Disagree with the idea, not the person. "I see your point, but…" is always welcome.

🏠 At home: free online lessons

📱 Independent English (B1): free

Every class has an online lesson with the same grammar, phrases, and conversations. It's your homework, and it helps you catch up if you miss a class. It works on a phone, a tablet, or a computer.

rays-esl.netlify.app/curriculum-b1/
  1. Open the website, or scan the QR code with your phone camera. 📷
  2. Choose the lesson your teacher gives you (for example, Lesson 2.3).
  3. Press the 🔊 buttons to hear the model sentences and conversations, then say them out loud. (No sound? Turn up the volume, or try the Chrome browser. The 🎧 Audio control lets you choose a US or UK voice and slow it down.)
  4. Do the exercises and the quiz, then try the speaking project. Record yourself on your phone, and listen back.

⏱ Try 30 minutes, 3 times a week. That's more useful than one long session. 🌟

The online lessons and our class both use American English (apartment, vacation, color). If you learned British English (flat, holiday, colour), that is correct too!
