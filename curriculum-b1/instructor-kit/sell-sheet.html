Teach Intermediate English to Adults, Ready to Run from Day One

Independent English (B1 Intermediate) is a complete 13-week classroom course for adults who have finished an elementary course or placed at B1: workers, parents, and newcomers who can manage everyday life in English and now need to explain, persuade, and speak up at work and in their community. Every class is planned to the minute. It works for a trained ESL teacher and for a first-time volunteer.

Why Programs Choose It

  • Zero prep to teach. Every class adds up to 90 minutes: warmer, presentation, drill, controlled practice, a real communicative task, and homework set-up. A volunteer can open the guide and teach.
  • English for work. Learners practice exactly what employers and case managers ask for: describing their experience and answering interview questions, making a complaint and getting a problem fixed, taking part in a discussion and disagreeing politely, reporting what a manager or customer said, and giving a short presentation. It fits WIOA and workforce-readiness goals.
  • Built for real adult classes. Mixed first languages, mixed literacy, and adults with busy, sometimes difficult lives. Each lesson gives literacy support and stretch options, and the tests have an adapted version. Personal tasks always offer a "new identity" or an imagined story, so nobody has to share what they'd rather keep private.
  • Discussion and fluency at its heart. B1 is where learners start to hold their own in a conversation. Every lesson ends in real speaking (debates, stories, role-plays, interviews), with fluency-before-accuracy feedback and clear routines that keep discussions fair.
  • The grammar of independence. Present perfect choices, narrative tenses, all the key conditionals and wishes, reported speech, relative clauses, the passive, modals of deduction, verb patterns, and phrasal verbs, taught through meaning and always put to work. Two review projects pull it together: a story-and-opinion task and a final presentation with a panel discussion.
  • A free companion course. Every lesson mirrors the free online course Independent English (B1) (rays-esl.netlify.app/curriculum-b1) with 🔊 audio on model sentences and dialogues. It serves as homework and review, and lets learners catch up after a missed class. A free tools page shows learners how to translate lessons into their own language.
  • A clear path forward. The course is the third step of a complete free CEFR ladder (A1 → C2). It follows Everyday English (A2), and learners who finish move into Confident English (B2), with Business English and IELTS preparation available alongside.
