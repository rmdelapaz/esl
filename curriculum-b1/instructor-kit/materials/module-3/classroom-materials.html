B · New Identity Cards (3.1 · 3.2 · 3.3)

Any learner may use a card instead of their own life, in any task: no reason needed. In 3.1 they report the "People told me" lines. In 3.2 they answer survey questions as the card person and can use the "People asked me" lines. In 3.3 they describe the people, places & things.

C · Gossip Chain — News Cards (3.1)

All the people live or work at the (invented) Maple Street apartment building. The first learner reads a card for 30 seconds and gives it back, then whispers a report ("Mrs. Lee said she was…"). Each learner passes it on. The last learner writes it. Score 1 point for each correct backshifted verb. The small gray line is the model report (for you); fold it back or cut it off before class.

News · 1

Mrs. Lee (apartment 4B)

"I'm moving to Florida next month. I've sold my car. My son will drive me there."

Mrs. Lee said she was moving to Florida the next month. She said she had sold her car and her son would drive her there. (3)

Carlos (the bus driver)

"I saw a famous singer on my bus yesterday! She was very friendly. I can't remember her name."

Carlos said he had seen a famous singer on his bus the day before. He said she had been very friendly and he couldn't remember her name. (3)

D · Survey Report Cards (3.2)

One card per learner; give neighbors different topics. Ask 3 people and write 1–3 words per answer. Then, in a new group, report: "I asked Omar what he usually ate for breakfast. He said he ate eggs." · "Lina asked me whether I liked spicy food. I told her I loved it." Every card mixes wh- and yes/no questions. Anyone can say "Pass!"

Survey · 1 · Food
  1. What do you usually eat for breakfast?
  2. Do you like spicy food?
  3. Can you cook a dish from another country?
  4. What is the best restaurant in this city?
  5. Have you ever tried sushi?
Survey · 2 · Weekends
  1. What do you usually do on Saturday mornings?
  2. Do you get up early on weekends?
  3. Where do you like to go on Sundays?
  4. Did you do anything fun last weekend?
  5. Are you going to do anything special this weekend?
Survey · 3 · Phones & apps
  1. How many hours a day do you use your phone?
  2. Do you have a favorite app?
  3. What was the last photo you took?
  4. Can you live one day without your phone?
  5. Do you prefer calls or text messages?
Survey · 4 · Learning English
  1. Why are you learning English?
  2. Do you watch TV or videos in English?
  3. What is the hardest thing about English?
  4. Have you done the online lesson this week?
  5. Will you take another English class next year?
Survey · 5 · Music & movies
  1. What kind of music do you like?
  2. Can you play an instrument?
  3. What was the last movie you saw?
  4. Do you sing in the car or the shower?
  5. Who is your favorite actor?
Survey · 6 · Work & dream jobs
  1. What was your first job? (or your dream job)
  2. Do you like working in a team?
  3. What time do you start work or class?
  4. Would you like to have your own business?
  5. Have you ever worked at night?
Report frame (literacy support · 1 per learner who needs it)
  • I asked ______ what / where / when / why he / she ______.
  • I asked ______ if / whether he / she ______.
  • He / She said (that) he / she ______.
  • ______ asked me if I ______. I told him / her (that) I ______.

E · Message Relay — Wall Cards (3.2)

Tape the 8 cards on the walls before class. Pairs: the runner reads one line (no card, no pen), walks back and reports it; the writer writes a short to-do on the "While you were out" note. Swap after 2 cards. The small gray lines are the model report and to-do for checking. All the messages are routine on purpose.

Message · 1 · 💼 Your boss (the restaurant manager)

"Please come in at eight tomorrow, not nine. Don't park in front of the restaurant. Can you work on Saturday? Bring your new uniform."

She asked you to come in at eight the next day. She told you not to park in front. She asked if you could work on Saturday. She told you to bring your new uniform. → To do: 8:00 start · park at the back · Saturday? · uniform
Message · 2 · 🩺 The doctor's office

"Your blood test is on Tuesday at 9. Don't eat anything after midnight. Please bring your insurance card. Do you need a ride to the clinic?"

They said your blood test was on Tuesday at nine. They told you not to eat after midnight. They asked you to bring your insurance card. They asked whether you needed a ride. → Tue 9:00 · no food after 12 · card · ride?
Message · 3 · 🏢 The building manager

"The plumber is coming on Thursday morning. Please move the things under your kitchen sink. Don't use the washing machine that morning. Is 10 o'clock OK for you?"

He said the plumber was coming on Thursday morning. He asked you to move the things under the sink. He told you not to use the washing machine that morning. He asked if ten o'clock was OK. → Thu a.m. · clear under sink · no washing · 10 OK?
Message · 4 · 🏫 Your child's school

"School finishes early on Friday, at 12:30. Please pick up your child at the side door. Don't forget the permission form for the museum trip. Can you help at the book sale?"

They said school finished early on Friday. They asked you to pick up your child at the side door. They reminded you to bring the permission form. They asked if you could help at the book sale. → Fri 12:30 side door · form · book sale?
Message · 5 · 💊 The pharmacist

"Your medicine is ready. Take one pill twice a day, with food. Don't drink alcohol with it. Do you have any allergies?"

She said your medicine was ready. She told you to take one pill twice a day with food. She warned you not to drink alcohol with it. She asked if you had any allergies. → pick up medicine · 1 pill ×2, with food · no alcohol · allergies?
Message · 6 · 🏠 Your neighbor

"I'm going away this weekend. Could you water my plants, please? Don't give the cat any milk. I'll leave the key under the mat."

She said she was going away that weekend. She asked you to water her plants. She told you not to give the cat any milk. She said she would leave the key under the mat. → water plants · no milk for cat · key under mat
Message · 7 · 🔧 The car mechanic

"Your car will be ready on Wednesday. Don't drive fast on the old tires. Please call before you come. Do you want a new battery too?"

He said your car would be ready on Wednesday. He warned you not to drive fast on the old tires. He asked you to call before you came. He asked whether you wanted a new battery. → car ready Wed · drive slowly · call first · battery?
Message · 8 · 🦷 The dentist's office

"We must change your appointment. Can you come on Monday at 4? Please arrive ten minutes early. Don't eat for an hour after the cleaning."

They said they had to change your appointment. They asked if you could come on Monday at four. They asked you to arrive ten minutes early. They told you not to eat for an hour after the cleaning. → new time Mon 4:00? · 3:50 · no food 1 hr after

F · Game — "Who Am I? / What Is It?" (3.3)

Rules (read them to the class, or give one copy per group)
  1. Groups of 3–4. Cards face down in the middle.
  2. Take a card. Define it without saying the word: "It's a person who… / It's a place where… / It's a thing which (that)… / You use it when…"
  3. The others guess with a full sentence: "Is it a nurse? A nurse is a person who helps doctors!" The first correct guess keeps the card. The winner of a card waits one turn before guessing again.
  4. Level 2: you can't say the "Don't say" words either.
  5. Most cards wins.

H · Listening & Dictation Scripts (teacher reads aloud)

Read at a natural speed, with pauses. Read each script twice: once for the main idea, once to check. Use contractions (I'm, I've, I'll), because that is what learners hear outside class. For the voicemails, hold a phone to your ear. The 🔊 buttons in the online lessons give learners extra listening at home.

3.1-A · Lina's voicemail (Workbook 3.1, Ex 1)

Hi, it's Lina! I have great news. I'm starting a new job next week! I've found a job at the hospital, in the kitchen. The hours are good. I can walk there from my apartment, and I'll finish at four every day, so I can pick up the kids from school. I saw the manager yesterday, and she was really nice. Oh, and I must buy some black shoes for work! Call me tonight. Bye!

Answers: 1 was starting · 2 had found · 3 were · 4 could · 5 would finish · 6 had seen · 7 had to. Follow-up: "What did she tell you to do?" (She told me to call her that night: a preview of 3.2.)

3.1-B · Dictation (read each sentence 3 times)

1. She said that she was tired after the trip. · 2. He told me that he had finished the report. · 3. They said they would arrive on Monday. · 4. My friend told me she couldn't come to the party.

3.2-A · At the clinic front desk (Workbook 3.2, Ex 3: two voices, or ask a confident learner to read Mr. Kim)

Receptionist: Good morning. Can I help you? · Mr. Kim: Hi. I'm here to see Dr. Brown. · R: Do you have an appointment? · K: Yes, I do. · R: What time is your appointment? · K: At ten thirty. · R: Have you been here before? · K: No, it's my first time. · R: OK. Can you spell your last name, please? · K: K-I-M. · R: Thank you. Do you have your insurance card? · K: Yes, here it is. · R: Great. Please fill out this form. And don't forget to sign it at the bottom. Please have a seat. The doctor will call you soon.

Answers: 1 if / whether he had · 2 his appointment was · 3 he had been · 4 to spell · 5 whether / if he had · 6 to fill out · 7 not to forget. Extra: She said the doctor would call him soon.

3.2-B · Voicemail from the building manager (live demo before the Message Relay)

Hi, this is Mr. Ortiz, the building manager. The plumber is coming on Thursday morning to fix your kitchen sink. Please be at home between nine and twelve. Can you move the things under the sink? And please don't use the washing machine that morning. Oh, and do you have any other problems in the apartment? Call me back. Thanks!

Model report (to a roommate): "Mr. Ortiz called. He said the plumber was coming on Thursday morning. He asked us to be at home between nine and twelve. He asked if we could move the things under the sink. He told us not to use the washing machine that morning. He asked whether we had any other problems, and he asked us to call him back."

3.3-B · Comma dictation (read each sentence 3 times; pause clearly at the commas)

1. My brother, who lives in Chicago, is a nurse. · 2. The man who lives next door is a doctor. · 3. Paris, which is the capital of France, is beautiful. · 4. That's the woman whose car was stolen.

Answers: commas in 1 and 3 only. Ask: "Why no commas in 2?" (It says which man.)
