5.1 · had + past participle

The same for everyone

✅I'd finished. · She had gone. · They'd left.
❌We hadn't met before that day.
❓Had you seen the email? · Why had he gone?
💬Yes, I had. / No, I hadn't.
PARTICIPLES gone · left · lost · forgotten · eaten · seen · taken · written
WATCH had forget ❌ · Had you did…? ❌ · Yes, I'd ❌ · 'd + go = would!
5.1 · Check the meaning

Which happened first?

"When I arrived, the train had left." Did I see the train? (No.) Which was first? (The train left.)
"When I arrived, the train left." Did I see the train? (Yes! It left as I arrived.)
"I was hungry because I hadn't eaten all day." Cause or result? (The cause: it came first.)
"I got up, had breakfast and left." Past perfect? (No need: a simple list in order.)
5.1 · Signal words

by the time · already · just · never … before

by the timeBy the time we arrived, the shop had closed.
before / afterHe had eaten before he left. · After she'd finished, she rested.
becauseHe couldn't log in because he'd forgotten his password.
had + ___ + ppThe bus had already gone. · I'd just sat down. · I'd never flown before.
SAY IT "the train had alREADy LEFT" · I'd /aɪd/ · hadn't /ˈhædənt/

5.1 · Group game · 20 min

🕵️ What Had Happened?

  • Reader: read the scene, show the clues. Hide the answer!
  • Detectives: ask "Had she lost her ticket?"
  • Reader: "Yes, she had. / No, she hadn't."
  • Find the answer → keep the card. 8 questions max.
  • One question per turn, clockwise.
See the answer? (No!)
First word of the question? ("Had.")
How many questions? (Eight.)
Who keeps the card? (The person who finds the answer.)
5.2 · Three tenses, one story

🎥 The story camera

🎬 actionpast simplemain events, in orderThe bus stopped. He got off.
🌧️ backgroundwas / were + -ingthe scene, in progressIt was raining.
⏪ flashbackhad + ppearlier than the storyHe'd left his umbrella at home.
BALANCE Mostly past simple · a little background · one or two flashbacks
5.2 · while & when · check the meaning

A long action + a short surprise

while + was -ingWhile we were eating, the lights went out.
when + past simpleI was walking home when I saw an old friend.
"While Dev was drinking his coffee, the bus stopped." Long action? (Drinking coffee.) Short and sudden? (The bus stopped.) "The bus was stopping"? (No: sudden = past simple.)
WATCH Suddenly the phone was ringing ❌ · While I walked… ❌ · I was knowing ❌

5.2 · Model dialogue · be a good listener

You'll never believe what happened!

A: So, I was walking to the station, and it was raining really hard. I'd forgotten my umbrella.

B: Typical! What did you do?

A: While I was standing under a tree, a car suddenly pulled up. It was my old teacher! I hadn't seen her for years.

B: No way! What a coincidence.

A: She offered me a ride, and in the end she drove me right to my door.

REACT No WAY! · Oh NO! · You're kidding! · Then what HAPpened? · How did it end?

5.3 · Which future?

Decide now · plan · in the diary

⚡ willdecide now · I think · offer · promise(The phone rings.) I'll get it! · I think it'll rain.

5.3 · will be + -ing

✈️ This time tomorrow, I'll be flying.

✅At 8 p.m. I'll be watching the game.
❌Don't call at noon. I won't be working then.
❓ politeWill you be using the car tonight?
"At 8 I'll watch the game." / "At 8 I'll be watching the game." Which starts at 8? (The first.) Which is already happening at 8? (The second.)
"Will you be using the car?" Pushy or polite? (Polite.)
WATCH I'll flying ❌ · I will be fly ❌
5.3 · Time clauses

No will after when

❌I'll call you when I will arrive.
✅I'll call you when I arrive.
✅I'll text you as soon as I land. · We'll wait until she comes.
✅When the class finishes, we'll go for coffee.
LIKE "IF" If I see her, I'll tell her. · When I see her, I'll tell her.
