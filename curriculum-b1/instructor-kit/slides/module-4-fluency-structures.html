4.1 · Why passive? · check the meaning

The thing is the star

❓ unknownMy car was stolen.
🤷 not importantThe rooms are cleaned every day.
👮 obviousHe was arrested on Friday.
🌍 everyoneRice is eaten all over Asia.
⚙️ a processThe beans are dried, then roasted.
"Hamlet was written by Shakespeare." Is by Shakespeare useful? (Yes! Famous.)
"My bike was stolen by someone." Useful? (No. Drop it.)
"This watch is made in Switzerland." About the workers or the watch? (The watch.)
4.1 · Active → passive in 3 steps

They built the house in 1990.

  • ① The object moves to the front: The house…
  • ② The verb becomes be + participle (same tense): …was built…
  • ③ The doer becomes by… or disappears: The house was built in 1990.

⚙️ First, the coffee cherries are picked. The skins are removed and the beans are dried in the sun. Then they are roasted and ground. Finally, the coffee is packed and sold.

4.1 · Team quiz · 14 min

🏭 How It's Made quiz

  • Teams of 3–4. Listen to the question and the 3 answers.
  • Talk for 30 seconds, in English.
  • The speaker says a full passive sentence: "Paper was invented in China."
  • 1 point for the fact · +1 for good English. New speaker every round.
Who talks first? (The team, 30 seconds.)
How do you answer? (A full passive sentence.)
Same speaker every time? (No, it changes.)
Wrong fact, perfect sentence? (Still 1 point!)

4.2 · The certainty scale

How sure are you?

✅ sure YESmustThe light's on, so she must be home.
🤔 maybemight · may · couldShe's not answering. She might be busy.
❌ sure NOcan'tHe can't be hungry. He just ate.
WATCH modal + plain verb · must to be ❌ · must is ❌ · It can be the mail carrier ❌ → might be
4.2 · Check the meaning

must: one word, two jobs

📜 "You must sign here."
A rule or a guess? (A rule.)
🕵️ "You must be tired."
A rule or a guess? (A guess from a clue.)
"He mustn't be at home. His car's gone."
Correct? (No!)
Sure NO = (can't): "He can't be at home."
mustn't = (not allowed)
SAY IT I CAN'T /kænt/ (strong) · I can /kən/ go (weak) · mustn't = "MUS-ent"
4.2 · Rules & advice · review

Do I have to? Should I?

📜 necessaryhave to / mustYou have to show ID at the bank.
🆓 not necessarydon't have toIt's free. You don't have to pay.
🚫 not allowedmustn't · US: can't / not allowed toYou mustn't (can't) park here.
💡 adviceshould / shouldn'tYou look tired. You should rest.
WATCH "You mustn't come" ≠ "You don't have to come" · a rule (have to) ≠ my advice (should)
4.2 · Model dialogue

Whose bag is it?

A: Someone left a bag on the train. Whose do you think it is?

B: There's a laptop and some business cards, so it can't be a child's.

A: And there's a ticket to Chicago. The owner might be going there for work.

B: Look, the coffee's still warm. They must be on the train right now!

A: They could be in the next car. We should tell the conductor.

4.2 · Groups · 13 min

🕵️ The detective agency

  • "Whose bag?": turn over one clue at a time
  • Detective cards: 3 deductions (must · might · can't) + the clue
  • Clue, so deduction: "The lights are off, so…"
  • The reader adds advice: "We should…"
Guesses about people in this room? (No! Pictures, objects, card people.)
All clues at once? (No, one at a time.)
The word between clue and deduction? ("So.")
4.3 · Opinions · claim + reason + example

Not only "I think…"

💬 give

🧱 support

For example, … · What's more, …
On the one hand … on the other hand …
WATCH I am agree ❌ · According to me ❌ · I think no ❌ → I don't think so
4.3 · Agree & disagree politely

Acknowledge first, then disagree

👍That's a good point. · Exactly! · I couldn't agree more.
🙏I see what you mean, but… · That may be true, but… · I'm not sure I agree.
🤝"I love cooking." — "So do I." · "I don't like crowds." — "Neither do I." · "I've been there." — "So have I."
"You're wrong." Polite? (No!) · "I see what you mean, but…" Agree or disagree? (Disagree, politely.)
4.3 · Groups of 4 · 15 min

🎤 The mini debate

  • Take a topic card and an opinion card (not your own opinion!)
  • Round 1: 1 minute each · Round 2: respond (3 talking tokens)
  • Start with the last speaker: "That's a good point, but…"
  • The chair keeps time, invites everyone, sums up
Your real opinion? (No, the card's.)
A token = ? (One turn.)
What does the chair do? (Time, invite, sum up.)
Before you disagree? ("I see what you mean, but…")
