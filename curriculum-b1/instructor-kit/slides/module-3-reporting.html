3.1 · Direct vs reported

Their words → your sentence

Direct (exact words)Reported (later, your words)
Mrs. Lee said, "I'm moving to Florida."Mrs. Lee said (that) she was moving to Florida.
She said, "I've sold my car."She said she had sold her car.
She said, "My son will drive me."She said her son would drive her.
Who is moving: me or Mrs. Lee? (Mrs. Lee.) Did she say "was"? (No, "I'm.") Is she moving in the past? (No! "was" is only because I'm reporting later.)

3.1 · Say vs tell · check the meaning

TELL needs a person

sayShe said (that) she was busy.
tellShe told me (that) she was busy.
chunkstell the truth · tell a story · tell a joke
"She told me she was busy." Who did she speak to? (Me.) · "She said me…"? (No! "She told me…") · "She told to me"? (No "to.")
WATCH said me ❌ · told to me ❌ · told that… (no person) ❌ · he said he is tired ❌
3.1 · Model · the original, then the report

"You'll never guess — I saw Sam!"

Sam: I'm moving to Chicago next month. I've found a great apartment.

Alex: Really? I didn't know that! I'll visit you there.

Sam: Please do. My sister lives there too. She can show you around.

Alex, later: "Sam told me he was moving to Chicago the next month and he had found a great apartment. I said I would visit him. He said his sister lived there too and could show me around!"

3.2 · Reported wh- questions

A reported question is not a question

DirectReported
"Where do you live?"She asked where I lived.
"What are you doing?"He asked what I was doing.
"Why did you leave?"They asked why I had left.
"When will the train arrive?"She wanted to know when the train would arrive.
RECIPE keep the wh- word → subject + verb → backshift → no do / did, no "?" · voice goes down ↘
3.2 · Yes/No questions · if / whether

No wh- word? Use if or whether

"Are you ready?"He asked if I was ready.
"Do you speak French?"She asked whether I spoke French.
"Can you swim?"He asked if I could swim.
"Have you finished?"She asked whether I had finished.
"He asked if I was ready." The question? ("Are you ready?") · A wh- word? (No.) · Question mark? (No!)
SAY IT asked = /æskt/ "askt" · whether = weather /ˈwɛðər/
3.2 · Requests & commands

tell / ask + person + (not) to + verb

"Sit down!"She told me to sit down.an order
"Please help me."He asked me to help him.a polite request
"Don't worry."She told me not to worry.negative
more verbsreminded me to call · warned us not to go · advised me to rest
WATCH told me don't worry ❌ · told me to not worry ❌ · said me to go ❌ · asked to me ❌

Two ideas → one sentence

3.3 · Defining vs non-defining · check the meaning

Which one? · or · an extra fact?

"My sister who lives in Rome is a lawyer."
How many sisters? (Two or more.)
Why "who lives in Rome"? (To say which one.)
Commas? (No.) "that" OK? (Yes.)
"My sister, who lives in Rome, is a lawyer."
How many sisters? (One.)
Is "who lives in Rome" needed? (No, extra.)
"My sister, that lives…"? (No! who / which only.)
SAY IT My sister | who lives in Rome | is a lawyer. — pause at the commas
3.3 · Drop it? · sentence strips · 12 min

✂️ Object: drop it · Subject: keep it

The movie (that) I watched was great.✂️ a new subject (I) comes next
The people (who) we met were lovely.✂️ OK
The man who called me was angry.🔒 keep it: he called
My car, which is very old, broke down.🔒 commas = always keep it
Strips: two sentences → how many? (One.) · Comma cards when? (The information is extra.) · "that" after a comma? (Never.)
