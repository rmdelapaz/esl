8.1 · Question tags · what are they?

A little question at the end

Plain statementStatement + tag
It's cold today.It's cold today, isn't it?
You know Maria.You know Maria, don't you?
He can't drive.He can't drive, can he?
"It's cold today, isn't it?" Information only, or an invitation to answer? (An invitation.) What answer do I expect? ("Yes, it is!")
8.1 · The mirror rule

Flip it. Copy the helper.

➕ → ➖You're tired, aren't you? · They've left, haven't they? · She'll help, won't she?
➖ → ➕He isn't ready, is he? · We can't park here, can we?
no helperYou live here, don't you? · She works late, doesn't she? · They went home, didn't they?

8.1 · Intonation drill · 12 min

Same words, two meanings

↘ voice goes down↗ voice goes up
I'm sure. Agree with me!I'm not sure. I'm checking.
Lovely day, isn't it? ↘You haven't seen my keys, have you? ↗
You're coming, aren't you? ↘You're coming, aren't you? ↗
"Lovely day, isn't it? ↘" Do I know the answer? (Yes.) Am I really asking? (No.)
"…have you? ↗" Do I know? (No. Tell me!)
"You don't drive, do you?" You don't. Answer? ("No, I don't.")
8.1 · Keeping a conversation going

React → Ask → Share

😮 ReactOh really? · That's interesting! · How nice! · Oh no! · You're kidding!
🔁 Echo"I went to Chicago." — Did you? · "She's moved." — Has she?
❓ Follow-upWhat was it like? · How long for? · Who did you go with? · How did it go?
🙋 ShareMe too! I… · Really? I've never…
👂 Backchannelmm-hm · uh-huh · right · yeah · really? · no way!
8.1 · Model dialogue

You went to the new café, didn't you?

Priya: You went to the new café on Bridge Street, didn't you?

Tom: I did, yeah. We had breakfast there on Sunday.

Priya: Oh really? What was it like?

Tom: Honestly, it was fantastic. The coffee's amazing.

Priya: Is it? I love a good coffee. It's not too expensive, is it?

Tom: Not really. You'll come with me next time, won't you?

Priya: Of course! Let's go on Saturday, shall we?

8.1 · Mingle · 20 min

Small-talk mingle

  • Read your card. Start with the tag ↘
  • 2 minutes: React → Ask → Share. 1 echo + 1 follow-up each
  • Bell → "Nice talking to you!" → swap cards → new partner
  • Round 4: guess & check with a tag ↗
Voice up or down at the start? (Down.)
How long? (Two minutes.)
What must you use? (An echo + a follow-up.)
No tokens left? (Only ask questions.)
Too personal? ("Let's talk about something else, shall we?")
8.2 · Phrasal verbs · what are they?

verb + particle = one meaning

Literal (easy)sit down · stand up · come in · pick up the box
Idiomatic (learn it)give up = stop trying · look after = take care of · find out = discover
Formal → everydaydiscover → find out · postpone → put off · continue → carry on · solve → sort out
"He gave up smoking." Does he smoke now? (No.) · "I ran into my teacher." Did I plan it? (No, by chance.)

8.2 · Separable or inseparable?

Split ✂️ · Stick 🔗 · No object 🚫

✂️ splitTurn on the TV. = Turn the TV on. → Turn it on. ✅ · Turn on it. ❌
🔗 stickI look after the kids. → I look after them. ✅ · I look them after. ❌
🚫 no objectI get up at seven. · Let's get together. · Carry on!
+ -ingI'm looking forward to seeing you. (not "to see")
SAY IT turn it off "tur-ni-DOFF" · pick them up "pick-em-UP" · stress the particle
8.2 · Group game · 15 min

Split or Stick?

  • Turn over a card. Say it with it / them: "Put them away!"
  • Place it: ✂️ SPLIT · 🔗 STICK · 🚫 NO OBJECT
  • Group disagrees? Test it: does it sound right?
  • Round 2: pronoun race. Fastest correct team wins a point.
Before you place a card? (Say it with a pronoun.)
How many places? (Three.)
"Look after the baby" → ? ("Look after her.")
"Throw away the milk" → ? ("Throw it away.")
8.3 · Your talk · 3–4 minutes

Intro → 2–3 points → conclusion

1 · startToday I'd like to talk about… · I chose this topic because…
2 · pointsFirst of all… · Another point is… · What's more… · For example… · Finally…
3 · endTo sum up… · In conclusion… · Thank you for listening. Any questions?
TOPIC a hobby · a skill · a place you love · a change you'd like to see · a topic card
8.3 · Model · a talk and a panel

Maya: why we should cycle to work

Host: Welcome, everyone. Maya, over to you.

Maya: Today I'd like to talk about… First of all, it's healthy. Another point is cost… Finally… To sum up…

Host: Let's open it up. Sam, what do you think?

Sam: That's a really good point about health. But it's not always safe, is it?

Maya: I see what you mean. However, if cities built more bike lanes, it would be much safer.

Priya: I'm not sure I agree that everyone can do it, though.

8.3 · Panel roles

🪑 Chair · 🎤 Speakers · 🙋 Audience

🪑 chairWelcome to our panel. · Let's start with… · What's your view on that? · One more question. · To wrap up, let's thank our speakers.
🎤 speakerIn my opinion… · That's a good point, but… · I see what you mean. However… · I'm not sure I agree, to be honest.
🙋 audienceI have a question for… · You said that… Could you explain…? · What would happen if…?
WATCH "No, you're wrong." 😬 → disagree with the idea, not the person
8.3 · The task · 18 + 14 min

Present, then panel

  • A · groups of 4: each talk 3–4 min, from key words
  • Listeners: 1 question + a ★ star and a 🎯 wish
  • B · fishbowl: 1 group = panel (chair + 3 speakers), 1 group = audience. 7 min, then swap
  • Speakers: 1 minute each · audience: 1 question each
How long is your talk? (3–4 minutes.)
Read a full script? (No, key words.)
Who opens and closes the panel? (The chair.)
Disagree how? ("I see your point, but…")
