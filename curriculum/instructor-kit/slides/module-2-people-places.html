Nice to meet you!

B: Hello, Leo. I'm Sofia. Nice to meet you.
A: Nice to meet you too. Where are you from, Sofia?
B: I'm from Mexico. I'm Mexican. And you?
A: I'm from Italy. I'm Italian.
B: Oh, nice! Is your friend Italian too?

The world-map mingle

  • Put a dot on any country: home, a country you love, a dream country
  • Be you, or take an identity card
  • Stand up. Meet 5 people: Where are you from? — I'm from… I'm…
  • Write the names and countries. Then tell your group: This is… She's from…
