7.1 · Can I count it?

an apple, two apples · two rices ✗

YES ✅ countable

  • an egg → three eggs
  • a banana → two bananas
  • How many?

NO ❌ uncountable

  • rice · milk · water · bread
  • no -s · no a / an
  • How much?
CCQ Can I count rice? · Do we say "a water"? · A glass of water: can I count the glasses?
7.1 · some / any

Look in the fridge

➕There's some milk.
There are some eggs.
➖There isn't any bread.
❓Is there any juice?
CCQ "There isn't any bread." Is there bread? (No.) Question: some or any? (Any.)
7.1 · How much? How many?

Look at the -s

can't count (milk)can count (eggs)
QuestionHow much milk is there?How many eggs are there?
👍 a big amountThere's a lot of milk. · There are a lot of eggs.
👎 a small amountThere isn't much milk.There aren't many eggs.
WATCH "There's much water" ✗ → "There's a lot of water" ✓
7.1 · Pair task · 12 min

The fridge: find 6 differences

  • A and B: don't show your card
  • Ask: Is there any…? Are there any…?
  • Ask: How much…? How many…?
  • Write one shopping list (5 things)
  • Can you show your card? (No.)
  • Milk: "Is there" or "Are there"? (Is there.)
  • What do you write at the end? (A list.)
ICQs Check before you start.
7.2 · At a café

What can I get you?

7.2 · Ordering politely

I'd like a coffee, please.

😐 direct🙂 polite
I want a coffee.I'd like a coffee, please. ↘
Give me a sandwich.Can I have a sandwich, please? ↗
  • I'd = I would · say the d: "I'd like"
CCQ "I'd like a coffee" = "I like coffee"? (No!) Which one is ordering now? (I'd like.)
7.2 · Model dialogue · listen & repeat

At the café

Leo: Hi there! What can I get you?

Maya: Hello. I'd like a coffee, please.

Leo: Would you like some milk with that?

Maya: Yes, please. And can I have a cheese sandwich, please?

Leo: Anything else? … For here or to go?

Maya: For here, please. How much is that?

Leo: That's $8.50, please.

Maya: Here you are. Keep the change.

7.2 · Offers & paying

Yes, please. · No, thank you.

  • Would you like some milk?
  • Anything else? → No, thanks. That's all.
  • For here or to go? (UK: eat in / take away)
  • How much is that?
  • It's $4.50: "four FIFty"
  • $4.15: "four fifTEEN"
  • Here you are. · Keep the change.
WATCH "How much it is?" ✗ → "How much is it?" ✓
7.2 · Role-play · 16 min

The classroom café

  • Server: order pad · greet · offer · price
  • Customer: card + menu · order · pay
  • Swap roles at the bell
  • Customers: what do you say first? (I'd like… please.)
  • Servers: what do you write? (The order.)
  • An offer: what do you answer? (Yes, please / No, thank you.)
ICQs Check before you open the café.

7.3 · this / these · shopping

I like this jacket. I like these shoes.

☝️ one✌️ two or more
This hat is nice.These jeans are nice.
Always plural: pants · jeans · shoes · socks · gloves
  • What size are you? → I'm a medium.
  • Can I try it on?
  • It's too big / too small.
  • How much is it? → I'll take it.
CCQ "These jeans": one pair or two? (One pair.) Why "these"? (Jeans are always plural.)
7.3 · What to wear

Wear a coat when it's cold.

🥶 It's cold.Wear a coat, a scarf, and gloves.
🌧️ It's raining.Take an umbrella.
🥵 It's hot.Wear a T-shirt.
❄️ It's snowing.Wear boots.
☀️ It's sunny.Wear a hat.
7.3 · Role-play · 16 min

The clothing store

  • Shoppers: read your card: weather · size · money
  • Visit 2 stores · try on · ask the price
  • Buy what the weather needs
  • Assistants: help · give sizes and prices
  • How much money do you have? (Look at your card.)
  • Can you buy everything? (No, only what you need.)
  • Before you buy, you ask…? (Can I try it on? How much is it?)
ICQs Then swap roles after 7 minutes.
