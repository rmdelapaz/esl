👂 Listen · 🗣️ Read with a partner

In a shop

Maya: Look at these bags! They're nice.

Leo: There are red bags, blue bags, and black bags. Which one do you like?

Maya: I like the big red bag. But it's expensive.

Leo: What about this small green one? It's cheap.

Maya: I don't like green. What colors are the other ones?

Leo: There are two brown bags and one gray bag.

Maya: A nice small gray bag. Perfect!

👥 Groups of 3–4 · 15 min

Describe & Guess

  • Take a card. Don't show it!
  • Say 3 clues: "It's yellow. It's long. It's a fruit."
  • Others ask: "Is it a banana?" — "Yes, it is!"
  • Boots, socks, gloves → "They're brown."
ICQs: Do you show your card? (No) · Do you say the word? (No!) · How many clues? (Three) · What do the others ask? ("Is it a…?")
5.2

Where Things Are

Where are my keys? — They're in your bag.

Prepositions of place

Where is the ball?

WATCH in front of = 3 words · on = touching · above = higher, not touching
Where is…? · Where are…?

One → is / It's · Many → are / They're

QUESTIONANSWER
Where is my phone?It's on the table.
Where are my keys?They're in your bag.
Where's the remote?Between the cushions.
AT at home · at work · at school (not "in home")
👥 Pairs · A and B · 15 min

Spot the Difference

  • A has Room A. B has Room B. Don't show your picture!
  • Ask: "Where is the cat?" "Where are the books?"
  • "In my room, it's under the table." — "Me too!" / "Oh! In my room, it's on the sofa."
  • Find 6 differences. Write them in the table.
ICQs: Do you show your picture? (No!) · Are the rooms the same? (No, a little different) · How many differences? (Six) · Talk or point? (Talk)

CCQ: "Yes, I have got": OK? (No, just "Yes, I have") · "She's got glasses": 's = is or has? (has) · "She's happy": 's = ? (is) · How do you know? (got!)
Describing people

She's got long dark hair.

  • hair: long · short · curly · straight · dark · blond · gray
  • eyes: blue · brown · green
  • glasses · a beard · a moustache
  • He's tall. (= he is)
WATCH long hair, not "long hairs"
👂 Listen · 🗣️ Read with a partner

At the station

Sam: I'm at the station. How can I find your brother?

Nadia: He's tall, and he's got short dark hair.

Sam: Has he got a beard?

Nadia: Yes, he has. And he's got glasses too.

Sam: Has he got a bag?

Nadia: Yes — he's got a big blue backpack.

Sam: Tall, dark hair, glasses… Is that him?

Nadia: That's him! His name is Omar.

👥 Pairs · A and B · 15 min

What's in your bag?

  • Look at your bag card. Don't show it!
  • Ask: "Have you got a key?" — "Yes, I have." / "No, I haven't." ✓ ✗
  • Find 2 things you've both got.
  • Tell another pair: "She's got a passport. She hasn't got a phone."
ICQs: Do you show your card? (No) · "Have you got" or "Has you got"? (Have you got) · Partner says "Yes, I have": what do you do? (Tick ✓) · How many things have you both got? (Two)
