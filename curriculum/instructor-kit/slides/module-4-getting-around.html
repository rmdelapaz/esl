Form · can / can't + verb

Same for everyone

Subjectcan / can'tverb

  • She cans swim → She can swim.
  • I can to swim → I can swim.
  • He can swims → He can swim.
Check · meaning & sound

"She can swim."

Is she able to swim? Yes.
Is she swimming now? We don't know.
"She cans swim." OK? No!
  • I c'n SWIM. (short, quiet)
  • I CAN'T SWIM. (long, strong)
  • Yes, I CAN!

At the café ☕

Server: Hello! Would you like a coffee?

Customer: Yes, please. And I'd like some water, too.

Server: Of course. Would you like some cake?

Customer: No, thank you. I don't like cake, but I love chocolate!

Server: Here you are.

Customer: Thank you!

POLITE "Yes, please." · "No, thank you." Just "yes" or "no" can sound rude.

"I'm working at home now." Is it every day? No. Is it now? Yes. Where do I work every day? In a bank.
Speaking · teams of 4–5 · 11 minutes

The mime game 🎭

  • Take a card. Don't show it!
  • Act it. No words.
  • Team: Are you cooking?
  • Actor: Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
  • Point: "She's cooking pasta!"
Show your card? No!
Actor, can you talk? Only "Yes, I am / No, I'm not."
How do you get a point? The full sentence.
4.3

Review Project: Shopping & Directions

Halfway through A1. Let's go out in town! 🎓

Shopping · how much?

How much is it? · How much are they?

Server: Hi! What would you like?

You: I'd like a coffee, please.

Server: Anything else?

You: A cookie, too. How much is that altogether?

Server: That's four dollars fifty.

You: Here you are. Can I pay by card?

  • 1 thing → How much is it? → It's $5.
  • 2+ → How much are they? → They're $10.
  • thirTEEN $13 · THIRty $30
  • receipt = ri-SEET
Directions · the imperative

Excuse me, where's the station?

  • ⬆ Go straight on.
  • ⬅ Turn left. ➡ Turn right.
  • Take the first left / the second right.
  • Go past the school.
  • It's on your left / right.
  • It's next to the bank.
  • It's opposite the café.
  • It's on the corner.
WATCH in the right → on your right · turn to the left → turn left
Task 1 · pairs · back to back · 15 minutes

Map A ⇄ Map B

  • Start at the ★
  • Ask: "Excuse me, where's the pharmacy?"
  • Listen. Write the place in the ? box.
  • Say it back: "So, first left, on my right?"
  • Then swap.
See your partner's map? No.
First word? "Excuse me…"
Where do you start? At the star.

The Corner Shop & the Market Stall 🛒
