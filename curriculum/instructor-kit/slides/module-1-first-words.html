Spelling out loud · capital · double

How do you spell it?

A: Hello! What's your name?

B: My name is Anna.

A: How do you spell it?

B: A – N – N – A. Anna.  (A – double N – A)

A: And your last name?

B: Costa. C – O – S – T – A.

A: Thank you!

NOTE Maria = capital M – a – r – i – a

Formal · informal

Two dialogues

🤝 Formal · at work

P: Good morning. My name is Ms. Park.

L: Good morning, Ms. Park. I'm David Lee. Nice to meet you.

P: Nice to meet you, too. How are you?

L: I'm fine, thank you. And you?

P: I'm very well, thank you. Please, come in.

😊 Informal · at school

M: Hi! I'm Maya.

S: Hi Maya, I'm Sam. Nice to meet you!

M: Nice to meet you, too. How are you?

S: I'm good, thanks. And you?

M: I'm good! See you later, Sam.

S: Bye, Maya!

WATCH Ms. Park = title + LAST name · not "Ms. Maria"
