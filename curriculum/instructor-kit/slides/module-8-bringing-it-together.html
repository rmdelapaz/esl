Pre-task · advice

You should rest.

  • You should rest.
  • You should drink water.
  • You should see a doctor.
  • You shouldn't go to work.
  • You shouldn't drink coffee.
  • Should I take medicine?
WATCH should + verb · "should to rest" ✗ · "should rests" ✗ · the l is silent: "shood"
Listening · at the doctor

What's the matter?

Doctor: Good morning. What's the matter?

Patient: I don't feel well. I have a headache and a sore throat.

Doctor: Does your stomach hurt?

Patient: No, it doesn't. But my back hurts a little.

Doctor: It's a cold. You should rest and drink a lot of water.

Patient: Should I take some medicine?

Doctor: Yes. And you shouldn't go to work for two days.

Pre-task · on the phone

I'd like to make an appointment.

Reception: Good morning, Riverside Health Center. How can I help you?

Sam: Hello. I'd like to make an appointment, please.

Reception: Can you come on Tuesday at 10:30?

Sam: Sorry, I work on Tuesday. What about Wednesday?

Reception: Wednesday at 2:15? · How do you spell your name?

Sam: S-A-M, L-E-E.

REVIEW on + day · at + time · spelling · I'd like…
Task 1 · groups of 3 · 14 min

The doctor's office

  • Patient: phone → problem → listen
  • Receptionist: day + time · spell the name
  • Doctor: one question · two pieces of advice
  • Clap = change roles
  • Your real problems? (No! From the card.)
  • Who talks first on the phone? (The receptionist.)
  • How much advice? (Two.)
HELP Sorry, can you say that again, please?

Model talk · listen: one hand or two?

My week

"Let me tell you about my week. I usually get up at six thirty. From Monday to Friday I work at a supermarket. In the evening I often cook dinner with my daughter. I never work on Sundays. Next week is different. On Monday I'm going to see the doctor. On Wednesday I'm going to meet my friend for coffee. And on Saturday I'm going to buy a warm coat!"

Task 2 · plan 6 min · tell 10 min

My Week talk

  • Plan: 4 every-week + 4 next-week sentences
  • Groups of 3: talk 1–2 minutes each
  • Listeners: ask one question
  • Your week or a week card. Both are great!
  • How many sentences? (8 or more.)
  • Next week: which grammar? (I'm going to…)
  • Listeners: what do you do? (Ask a question.)
Language focus · let's fix it together

Good English, better English

  • You should to rest. → You should rest. ✓
  • My legs hurts. → My legs hurt. ✓
  • I have cold. → I have a cold. ✓
  • I going to visit… → I'm going to visit… ✓
  • My son play soccer. → My son plays soccer. ✓
