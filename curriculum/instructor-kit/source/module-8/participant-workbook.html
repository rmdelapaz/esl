My … hurts (one)My back hurts. My tooth hurts.

Dialogue · 📖 Read · 👥 Practice with a partner

Doctor: Good morning. Come in and sit down. What's the matter?

Patient: I don't feel well. I have a headache and a sore throat.

Doctor: I see. How do you feel today?

Patient: Not good. I have a temperature, and I feel tired.

Doctor: Does your stomach hurt?

Patient: No, it doesn't. But my back hurts a little.

Doctor: It's a cold. It's not serious. You should rest and drink a lot of water.

Patient: Should I take some medicine?

Doctor: Yes, take this for your temperature. And you shouldn't go to work for two days.

Patient: Thank you, doctor. I feel better already!
