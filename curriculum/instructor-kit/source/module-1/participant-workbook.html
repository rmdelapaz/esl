Dialogues · 👂 Listen · 👥 Read with a partner

1 — Formal (at work)

Ms. Park: Good morning. My name is Ms. Park.

Mr. Lee: Good morning, Ms. Park. I'm David Lee. Nice to meet you.

Ms. Park: Nice to meet you, too. How are you?

Mr. Lee: I'm fine, thank you. And you?

Ms. Park: I'm very well, thank you. Please, come in.

Mr. Lee: Thank you.

2 — Informal (at school)

Maya: Hi! I'm Maya.

Sam: Hi Maya, I'm Sam. Nice to meet you!

Maya: Nice to meet you, too. How are you?

Sam: I'm good, thanks. And you?

Maya: I'm good! See you later, Sam.

Sam: Bye, Maya!

Grammar — age
QuestionAnswer
How old are you?I'm 25.  /  I'm 25 years old.
How old is he?He's 30.
How old is she?She's 19.

✅ I'm 25.    ❌ I have 25 years.

Don't want to say? That's OK: "Sorry, that's private."

Phone: one digit at a time. 0 = "oh". 55 = "double five".
Email: @ = "at"  ·  . = "dot" → ana@mail.com = "ana at mail dot com"

Dialogue · 👂 Listen · 👥 Read with a partner

A: What's your phone number?

B: It's oh – seven – nine – double four – two – three – one – oh – five – six.

A: Slowly, please!

B: Sure. Oh – seven – nine … four – four … two – three – one … oh – five – six.

A: Got it. And your email?

B: It's ben, B – E – N, at mail dot com.

A: Thank you!
