📖 🗣️ Dialogue — read with a partner

Sam: What time do you get up?

Mia: I get up at six. I take a shower and have breakfast. What about you?

Sam: Oh, I don't get up early. I wake up at eight!

Mia: Eight! Do you have breakfast?

Sam: No, I don't. I only drink coffee. Then I go to work.

Mia: I come home at six in the evening.

Sam: Me too. In the evening I cook dinner and watch TV.

📖 🗣️ Dialogue — read with a partner

A: What time do you start work?

B: I start at eight in the morning. And I finish at four.

A: And your brother? What time does he start?

B: He starts work at nine. He works in a hospital.

A: Does he work on Sunday?

B: No, he doesn't. He rests on the weekend. But he works on Saturday.

A: When does he get up?

B: Early! He gets up at six thirty, and he has breakfast at seven.

📖 🗣️ Dialogue — read with a partner

Tom: Wow, your new apartment is nice! How many rooms are there?

Sara: Thanks! There are four rooms. There's a living room, a kitchen, a bedroom, and a bathroom.

Tom: Is there a balcony?

Sara: Yes, there is! It's behind the living room. Come and see.

Tom: Are there any plants on it?

Sara: There are some plants, yes, between the two chairs.

Tom: Is there a TV?

Sara: There's a TV on the wall, and there's a sofa in front of it.

Tom: Is there a garage?

Sara: No, there isn't. But there's an elevator, so it's easy.
