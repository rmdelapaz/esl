Grammar box — where does the word go?
Normal verb: word before the verbVerb be: word after be
I always drink coffee.I am always tired.
She never eats meat.She is never late.
They usually work late.They are usually busy.

Remember: be after, verb before.   never = no. Say "I never smoke." Not "I don't never smoke."

👥 Dialogue — read with a partner

Ben: You look tired, Lucy. How often do you work at night?

Lucy: I'm a nurse, so I usually work at night. About three times a week.

Ben: Three times a week! Do you sleep in the day, then?

Lucy: Yes, I always sleep in the morning. But I'm often tired.

Ben: I'm a teacher, so I never work at night. I start at eight every morning.

Lucy: Lucky you! How often do you exercise?

Ben: I sometimes go running, maybe once a week. I hardly ever go to the gym.

👥 Dialogue — read with a partner

Nina: What do you do in your free time, Omar?

Omar: I love cooking. I make dinner every night. Do you like cooking?

Nina: No, I don't. I hate cooking! But I don't mind washing the dishes.

Omar: So what hobbies do you like?

Nina: I really enjoy painting, and I love hiking. I go every weekend.

Omar: Hiking! I like it too. Do you like swimming?

Nina: Not really. The water is too cold for me.

👥 Dialogue — read with a partner

Maya: Hi Leo! Are you free on Saturday?

Leo: On Saturday? Yes, I think so. Why?

Maya: There's a new café in town. Would you like to have lunch?

Leo: Sure, that sounds great! What time?

Maya: Let's meet at one o'clock. I'm going to walk there.

Leo: Perfect. I'm meeting my brother in the morning, but I'm free in the afternoon.

Maya: Great. And it's my birthday on Sunday, so I'm having a small party.

Leo: Happy early birthday! I'd love to come. See you at one on Saturday!
