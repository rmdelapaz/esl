Grammar box — can / can't
✅ Yes❌ No❓ QuestionShort answer
I can swim.I can't drive.Can you swim?Yes, I can. / No, I can't.
She can sing.She can't dance.Can she cook?Yes, she can. / No, she can't.
They can cook.They can't swim.Can they drive?Yes, they can. / No, they can't.

⚠️ can never changes: she cans → she can. No to: I can to swim.

😍🙂😐😖❓ Question
I love chocolate.I like tea.I don't like fish.I hate onions.Do you like tea? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.

👥 Dialogue — At the café

Server: Hello! Would you like a coffee?

You: Yes, please. And I'd like some water, too.

Server: Of course. Would you like some cake?

You: No, thank you. I don't like cake, but I love chocolate!

Server: Here you are. One coffee and some water.

You: Thank you!

Grammar box — am / is / are + -ing
✅❌❓
II'm eating.I'm not eating.Am I eating?
he / she / itShe's reading.She isn't reading.Is she reading? — Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
you / we / theyThey're playing.They aren't playing.Are they playing? — Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.
Every day 📅Right now ⏰
I work in a bank. (usually, every day)I'm working at home now. (now, at the moment)

👥 Dialogue — A phone call

A: Hi! What are you doing?

B: I'm cooking dinner.

A: Oh, nice! Is Maria there?

B: Yes, she's watching TV. And the kids are doing their homework.

A: Are you busy? Can I call later?

B: No problem. I'm not busy. Let's talk now!

👥 Now change the bold words. Talk about a home right now (your home or a pretend home).

🗣️ Speaking — Mime game

Take a card. Don't show it! Act it. Your team asks: "Are you cooking?" Answer: "Yes, I am." / "No, I'm not." Then the team says: "She's cooking!"

👥 Dialogue — Asking the way

Tourist: Excuse me, where's the train station?

Local: Go straight ahead and take the second left.

Tourist: The second left. Okay.

Local: Then go past the park. It's on your right, opposite a hotel.

Tourist: Is it far?

Local: No, about five minutes.

Tourist: Thank you very much!

Local: You're welcome. Have a good trip!
