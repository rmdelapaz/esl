Dialogue · 📖 Read · 👥 Practice with a partner

Nadia: Is there any rice?

Ben: Yes, there's some rice. But there isn't much.

Nadia: How many eggs are there?

Ben: Only two. That's not many.

Nadia: Is there any milk?

Ben: No, there isn't any milk.

Nadia: OK. We need some milk and some eggs. Put them on the list.

Grammar box
You want to…Say
orderI'd like a coffee, please.  (I'd = I would)
Can I have a sandwich, please?
answer an offerWould you like some milk? → Yes, please. / No, thank you.
stop orderingAnything else? → No, thank you. That's all.
ask the priceHow much is that? → It's $4.50. ("four fifty")
payHere you are.  ·  Keep the change.
Be careful

I'd like a coffee = I want a coffee now.  ·  I like coffee = I like it every day.

Dialogue · 📖 Read · 👥 Practice with a partner

Leo: Hi there! What can I get you?

Maya: Hello. I'd like a coffee, please.

Leo: Sure. Would you like some milk with that?

Maya: Yes, please. And can I have a cheese sandwich, please?

Leo: Of course. Anything else?

Maya: A piece of cake and a bottle of water, please.

Leo: For here or to go?

Maya: For here, please. How much is that?

Leo: That's $8.50, please.

Maya: Here you are. Keep the change.

Leo: Thank you! Enjoy your coffee.

Speaking · 👥 The classroom café

One person is the server. Take a menu and a customer card. Order, answer, ask the price, and pay. Then change roles.

What can I get you?I'd like…, please.Can I have…, please?Would you like…?Yes, please.No, thank you.How much is that?Here you are.

Emma: Yes, please. I like this jacket.

Emma: I'll take it. It's cold outside!
