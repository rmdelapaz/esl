4.1 Game — Find Someone Who Can…

Stand up. Ask "Can you…?" or "Do you like…?" If the answer is yes, write the name. One name in each box. Then ask one more question.

can swim
can cook rice
can play the guitar
can sing a song in English
can dance
can play football
🚗 can drive
🚲 can ride a bike
🗣️ can speak three languages
likes coffee
doesn't like fish
loves chocolate
Teacher

Model the question for the "doesn't like" box: you ask "Do you like fish?", and a "No, I don't" gets the name. Extra question ideas: What can you cook? · What languages can you speak? · Tea or coffee? Feedback in the 3rd person: Ana can swim. Omar doesn't like fish.

Listening & Dictation Scripts

4.1 · "Can or can't?" (Stage 4)

Learners show ✅ (thumbs up) for can and ❌ (thumbs down) for can't. Read at natural speed: weak c'n, strong CAN'T. Answers in brackets.

1. I can swim. (✅) · 2. She can't drive. (❌) · 3. We can dance. (✅) · 4. He can't cook. (❌) · 5. They can play football. (✅) · 6. I can't sing. (❌) · 7. My brother can ride a bike. (✅) · 8. You can't use my phone! (❌)
Dictation (write it): 1. I can cook, but I can't drive. · 2. Can you swim? · 3. Yes, I can. · 4. I don't like fish. · 5. Do you like tea?

4.2 · "Every day or now?" (Stage 9)

Learners hold up 📅 (every day) or ⏰ (now), or write E or N.

1. I work in a bank. (📅) · 2. Shh! The baby's sleeping. (⏰) · 3. She drinks tea every morning. (📅) · 4. Look! It's raining. (⏰) · 5. We usually walk to school. (📅) · 6. They're playing football right now. (⏰) · 7. He's wearing a red coat today. (⏰) · 8. My mother cooks on Sundays. (📅)
"Point to the picture" (flashcards on the board): She's cooking. · He's swimming. · They're dancing. · I'm drinking coffee. · She's reading. · He's watching TV. · They're singing. · She's talking on the phone.

4.3 · Prices dictation (workbook Ex 2)

Read each line twice. Stress: fifTEEN / FIFty, thirTEEN / THIRty.

1. The coffee? It's two dollars fifty. · 2. This bag is fifteen dollars. · 3. The shoes? They're fifty dollars. · 4. A sandwich is six dollars fifty. · 5. The umbrella? It's thirteen dollars. · 6. A coffee and a sandwich… that's nine dollars altogether.

4.3 · Mini-map directions (workbook Ex 4, items 5–8)

Learners start at the ★ on the workbook map. Read each twice. Pause for learners to trace the route with a finger.

5. Take the first right. It's on your right. (supermarket) · 6. Go straight on. It's on your left. (bank) · 7. Take the first left. It's on your left. It's green! (park) · 8. Go straight on, past Hill Street. It's on your right, opposite the school. (hospital)

4.3 · Model "A Day Out" (read with a strong learner before Task 3)

A: Hi! I'm Lina. I'm from Canada. Nice to meet you.

B: Nice to meet you too! I'm Omar. What would you like?

A: I'd like a tea and a cake, please. How much is that altogether?

B: That's five dollars. Cash or card?

A: Cash. Here you are. Excuse me, where's the station?

B: Take the first right. It's on your left.

A: So, first right, on my left? Thank you very much!

B: You're welcome. Have a nice day!
