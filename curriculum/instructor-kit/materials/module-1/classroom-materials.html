5 · Spelling cards A/B (C1)

Pairs sit back to back, or hold the card so the partner can't see it. A spells a word; B writes it. Then B spells; A writes. At the end, compare. Say "Can you repeat, please?" as often as you like.

Student A · you spell

Spell these to your partner

1. book   2. window   3. coffee

4. Maria   5. Lopez

Now write B's words:

1 ________ 2 ________ 3 ________

4 ________ 5 ________

Student B · you spell

Spell these to your partner

1. pencil   2. door   3. hello

4. Ahmed   5. Kim

Now write A's words:

1 ________ 2 ________ 3 ________

4 ________ 5 ________

Reminder for pairs: names → "capital M." Two the same → "double." (coffee = c – o – double f – double e)

7 · Meeting cards A/B (C2)

Look at the card, then put it down and speak. Say: greeting → name → "Nice to meet you" → "How are you?" → goodbye. Use a new-identity name if you like.

1 · A · Formal

☀️ 9:00 a.m. · New job

It's your first day at work. Meet your boss, Mr. Diaz.

Start: "Good morning, Mr. Diaz. I'm…"

1 · B · Formal

☀️ 9:00 a.m. · You are the boss

You are Mr. Diaz (or Ms. Diaz). A new worker comes. Ask the name. At the end say "Please, come in."

2 · A · Informal

🌤 2:00 p.m. · English class

A new classmate sits next to you. Say hi. Ask the name. How do you spell it?

2 · B · Informal

🌤 2:00 p.m. · English class

You are new in class. Say hi. Say your name and spell it. Say goodbye: "See you tomorrow!"

3 · A · Formal

🌆 7:00 p.m. · Your child's school

You meet the teacher, Ms. Park. Greet her. Say your name. Ask "How are you?"

3 · B · Formal

🌆 7:00 p.m. · You are the teacher

You are Ms. Park. A parent comes to meet you. Answer: "I'm very well, thank you. And you?"

4 · A · Informal

🌙 11:00 p.m. · A party

You meet a new friend at a party. Talk. Then it's late. You go home to bed. Say goodbye.

4 · B · Informal

🌙 11:00 p.m. · A party

Meet a new friend. Say your name. When your friend leaves, say "Good night! See you later."

11 · Read-aloud scripts (teacher)

How to read

Read each item twice, at natural speed and clearly, with a short pause. If learners say "Can you repeat, please?" or "Slowly, please," always do it, and praise them for asking.

Script 1.1 C · "Spell It, Find It" game — caller list

Round 1 (objects): c – h – a – i – r (chair) · n – o – t – e – b – o – o – k (notebook) · p – e – n – c – i – l (pencil) · t – e – a – c – h – e – r (teacher) · w – i – n – d – o – w (window)
Round 2 (names, stretch): capital M – a – r – i – a · capital A – h – m – e – d · capital K – i – m · capital L – o – p – e – z · capital A – double n – a

Script 1.2 · "Which greeting?" (C2 listening)

Learners hold up the greeting card they hear, or say "hello" or "goodbye." Then ask: "Formal or informal?"

1. (It's 8 in the morning.) — Good morning, Mr. Diaz. — Good morning, Rosa. How are you?
2. (It's 3 in the afternoon.) — Hi, Sam! — Hi, Maya! How are you? — I'm good, thanks.
3. (It's 7 in the evening.) — Good evening. My name is Ms. Park. — Good evening, Ms. Park. I'm David Lee. Nice to meet you.
4. (It's 11 at night.) — OK, I'm going home. Good night! — Good night! See you tomorrow.
5. (Any time.) — Hello! I'm Ana. — Hello, Ana. I'm Ben. Nice to meet you. — Nice to meet you, too.

Answers: 1 Good morning (formal) · 2 Hi (informal) · 3 Good evening (formal) · 4 Good night = goodbye (informal) · 5 Hello (either).
