7.1 · The Fridge (information gap)

One A and one B per pair. Learners ask Is there any…? / Are there any…? / How much…? / How many…? and circle the 6 differences. Teacher key: milk, eggs, cheese, apples, juice, butter are different. Rice, bread, tomatoes, coffee are the same.

Student A · my fridge

Ask about B's fridge. Don't show this card!

  • milk: ✅ a lot
  • eggs: ✅ 2 (not many)
  • rice: ✅ some
  • cheese: ❌ no
  • apples: ✅ 6
  • juice: ❌ no
  • bread: ✅ a lot

Listening & Dictation Scripts (teacher reads aloud)

Read naturally but a little slowly. Read each script twice: first for the main idea, then for details. Pause between lines for dictation.

Script 7.1 · Dictation: Ben's shopping list (C20, after the fridge task)

We need some milk. · We need six eggs. · There isn't any bread. · How much rice is there? · There aren't many apples. · Let's buy some fruit.

Learners write the six sentences in their notebook. Literacy support: learners draw or circle the food on a picture strip instead.

Script 7.2 A · At the café (Workbook 7.2, Ex 1)

Leo: Hi there! Welcome. What can I get you?
Maya: Hello. I'd like a coffee, please.
Leo: Sure. Would you like some milk with that?
Maya: Yes, please. And can I have a cheese sandwich, please?
Leo: Of course. Would you like anything else?
Maya: Yes, a piece of cake, please. Oh, and a bottle of water.
Leo: Great. For here or to go?
Maya: For here, please. Is there a table for two?
Leo: Yes, there's one by the window. How about that?
Maya: Perfect, thank you. How much is that?
Leo: That's eight fifty, please.
Maya: Here you are. Keep the change.
Leo: Thank you very much! Enjoy your coffee.

Gist question (1st listening): For here or to go? (For here.) Detail (2nd): circle the food and drinks and write the price ($8.50).

Script 7.2 B · Prices (Workbook 7.2, Ex 4)

1. A coffee? That's three fifty. · 2. The orange juice is two seventy-five. · 3. A muffin and a tea: four fifteen, please. · 4. Two salads… that's twelve twenty. · 5. That's six forty, please.

Answers: $3.50 · $2.75 · $4.15 · $12.20 · $6.40. Then say "four fifteen / four fifty" in random order; learners hold up a "15" or a "50" card they write on scrap paper.

Script 7.3 A · In the clothing store

Omar: Hello! Can I help you?
Emma: Yes, please. I like this jacket. It's really nice.
Omar: It's a lovely jacket. What size are you?
Emma: I'm a medium. Can I try it on?
Omar: Of course. The fitting room is over there.
Emma: Hmm. It's a bit too big. Do you have a small?
Omar: Yes, here you are. And these shoes go well with it.
Emma: Oh, I like these shoes too! How much is the jacket?
Omar: The jacket is thirty-five dollars, and the shoes are forty.
Emma: The jacket is perfect. I'll take it. It's cold outside, so I need it today!

Questions: What does Emma buy? (the jacket) · What size is the jacket she takes? (small) · How much is it? ($35) · What's the weather like? (cold)

Script 7.3 B · The weather report

Good morning! Here's the weather for this week. On Monday, it's sunny and warm. On Tuesday, it's cloudy. On Wednesday, it's raining all day, so take an umbrella! On Thursday, it's windy and cool. On Friday, it's cold. Wear a coat and a scarf. And on the weekend… it's snowing! Wear your boots.

Learners draw the weather symbol under each day (Mon–Sat/Sun) on scrap paper. Check: Wednesday? (raining, umbrella) · Friday? (cold, coat and scarf) · the weekend? (snowing, boots). Recycles days of the week from Module 3.

Game · Food & Clothes Bingo

Half the class gets card A, half card B. Read a sentence from the caller's list in any order. Learners cover the picture they hear (use coins or paper squares). First to cover a line says "Bingo!" and must read back the sentences for that line. Then play for a full card.
