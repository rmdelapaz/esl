6.1 · Listening Script — Lucy's week (Workbook Ex 4)

Read twice at natural A1 speed. Pause after each sentence the second time.

Hi, I'm Lucy. I'm a nurse. I usually work at night, three times a week. After work, I always sleep in the morning. I often cook dinner at home. I love it! I sometimes go swimming on Sundays. I hardly ever watch TV. I'm too tired! And I never drink coffee at night.

6.2 · Listening Script — Omar's family (Workbook Ex 4)

My name is Omar. I love cooking. I cook every night. My wife loves reading, but she hates cooking! My son likes playing soccer. He doesn't like reading. My daughter loves dancing. She dances every day. And my father? He enjoys gardening. He doesn't mind shopping. He goes to the market with me on Saturdays.

6.2 · Dictation — -ing words (optional warmer, C19)

Write the -ing word. One: swimming. Two: dancing. Three: cooking. Four: running. Five: making. Six: shopping.

Answers: swimming (mm) · dancing (no e) · cooking · running (nn) · making (no e) · shopping (pp).

6.3 · Information Gap — Diary Cards (one set per pair)

A and B sit face to face. Don't show your card! Ask: "Are you free on Monday morning?" or "What are you doing on Tuesday evening?" Mark your partner's busy times with ✗. Find the 3 times you are both free, then plan 3 things together.

Model exchange (teacher demo with a strong learner)

A: Are you free on Monday evening? B: Sorry, I'm busy. I'm cooking for my family. A: OK. Are you free on Wednesday evening? B: Yes, I am! A: Great! Would you like to have dinner at a restaurant? B: Sure, that sounds great! What time? A: Let's meet at seven o'clock. B: See you then!

Answer: both free on Wed 3rd evening, Sat 6th afternoon, Sun 7th afternoon.

6.3 · Listening Script — Dates dictation (Workbook Ex 4A)

Learners write month / day in numbers. Mix the two ways of saying a date on purpose.

Number one: June first. Number two: March the twenty-second. Number three: July fourth. Number four: the thirteenth of September. Number five: November thirtieth. Number six: February the twenty-first.

Answers: 6/1 · 3/22 · 7/4 · 9/13 · 11/30 · 2/21. Watch thirteenth vs thirtieth: stress thir-TEENTH vs THIR-ti-eth.

6.3 · Listening Script — A phone invitation (Workbook Ex 4B)

Hi Sam, it's Rosa. Are you free on Friday? There's a new film at the movie theater. Would you like to come? It starts at seven thirty. Let's meet at seven o'clock at the bus stop. I'm going to buy the tickets. Call me back! Bye!

Answers: Friday · see a film / go to the movies · starts 7:30 · meet 7:00 at the bus stop.
