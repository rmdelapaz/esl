Instructor-Led Course Syllabus

Confident English (B2 Upper-Intermediate)

A 13-week, 26-class English course for adults who already use English independently and want to sound confident. Twice a week for 90 minutes, you learn to tell layered stories, argue a case and handle disagreement, switch between formal and informal English, follow fast natural speech, and take part in a structured debate. Between classes, the free online lessons (with audio) are your homework.

Class Schedule

Two classes a week, 90 minutes each. Every class has one lesson from the online course, except the two assessment classes. Lessons 4.3 and 8.3 are review projects: a present-argue-and-discuss task at the halfway point, and a structured debate at the end. ✓ = the can-do goal for that class.

Week 1

Layering the Past

Module 1: telling stories in layers, and imagining how the past could have been different.
C11.1Narrative Tenses — Past Perfect & Continuouspast simple, past continuous, past perfect, past perfect continuous · by the time, just as, until✓ I can tell a story that moves back and forth in time.
C21.2The Third Conditional — Past HypotheticalsIf I had…, I would have… · could / might have · second vs third · mixed conditionals✓ I can talk about how things could have turned out differently.
Week 2

Regrets & Verb Patterns

Modules 1–2: regrets and preferences, then which verb form follows which.
C31.3Past Regrets — Wish, If Only, Would Rather & It’s Timewish + past perfect · wish + would · I’d rather you didn’t… · it’s (high) time we left✓ I can express regrets, preferences, and things that are overdue.
C42.1Gerunds & Infinitivesenjoy -ing vs decide to · preposition + -ing · purpose · remember, stop, try, regret, mean✓ I can talk about my habits, goals, and memories with the right verb pattern.
Week 3

Week 5

Course Details

Prerequisites

  • CEFR B1. You can tell a story in the right past tenses, give and defend an opinion, report what people said, and handle work situations such as a complaint or a job interview.
  • Finished Independent English (B1)? You’re ready. New to our program? Take the shared placement test first.
  • Learners who are still building basic reading and writing are welcome. Every lesson has literacy-support options, and the tests have an adapted version.
  • No computer needed in class. A phone helps for homework, but it isn’t required.

What’s Provided

  • A Student Workbook for all 24 lessons, in natural B2 English (the learner keeps it)
  • A welcome handout with class and debate ground rules, what to bring, and QR codes to the online lessons
  • For the teacher: a Teacher’s Guide (a 90-minute plan for every class), slides, flashcards, role-play and debate cards, listening scripts, games, and answer keys
  • Free access to the companion online course: Confident English (B2), with 🔊 audio
  • A free learning-tools page that shows how to translate lessons into any language

Assessment

  • Every class: an “I can…” checklist and short speaking checks, with no grades
  • Class 13 · Progress Test 1 (Modules 1–4): listening, vocabulary, grammar, reading, a reporting message, an argumentative paragraph, and a 7-minute pair speaking task with a short debate
  • Class 26 · Final Test (all 8 modules): the same skills plus fast natural speech, a formal email and a balanced argument essay, and an 8–10 minute individual interview with a long turn, a discussion with push-back, and a register role-play
  • Rubrics follow the CEFR B2 descriptors. Results decide the next class, never who gets to celebrate
  • Tasks about your life can always use a “new identity” or an imagined story, and in debates you may argue an assigned side instead of your own view

Homework & the Online Course

  • After each class, do the matching online lesson at home: about 30–40 minutes, with 🔊 Listen buttons for model sentences and dialogues
  • Plus one workbook page to review the class
  • Missed a class? The online lesson covers the same language, so you can catch up
  • After the course: Advanced English (C1) continues the same free ladder, all the way to C2. Business English and IELTS Preparation can run alongside

What You’ll Be Able to Do

By the end of the course, learners reach CEFR B2 and can use English with confidence, precision, and style, at work and in daily life:

Tell a layered story that moves back and forth in time
Talk about how the past could have been different, with regrets and preferences
Reason about what must, might, or can’t have happened
Report facts, beliefs, and conversations in a precise, formal style
Build a balanced argument with accurate linkers and emphasis
Describe future milestones, services you arrange, and groups with precision
Build the right word form and compare options with nuance
Ask politely in any situation, and switch between formal and informal English
Tell an engaging anecdote and give a persuasive presentation
Follow fast natural speech, and take part in a structured debate
