Welcome to Confident English! 👋

Welcome, and thank you for joining us. You already use English independently at work and in daily life. In this course you'll learn to tell stories with depth and drama, argue a case and handle disagreement, switch smoothly between formal and informal English, and follow fast, natural speech, so that you sound not just correct, but confident.

Some of you finished our B1 class, and some of you are joining us for the first time. Everyone brings experience and ideas to this room, and everyone is welcome.

⚖️ How we debate

At B2 we argue, persuade, and debate a lot, about work, our community, and everyday choices. Strong opinions make a class lively, so here's how we keep debates respectful and useful:

  • 👂Listen to the end. Let people finish their point before you answer it.
  • 💛Challenge the idea, not the person. "I'd question that assumption," never "That's silly."
  • 🤝Concede, then counter. Admit what's true in the other view first: "That's a fair point, but…"
  • ⚖️Share the floor. If you've spoken a lot, invite someone else in: "What's your take on this?"
  • 🎭Assigned sides are normal. You may argue a side you don't believe. It's great practice, and nobody has to share a personal view.
  • 🚪Some topics stay outside. We debate everyday, work, and community questions, not politics or religion.

When you need help

👔 Is there a more formal way to say ___?
In a discussion or debate
💭 As I see it, … / It seems to me that …
🤝 That's a fair point, but …
🎭 Just to play devil's advocate, …
🙌 What's your take on this?

🏠 At home: free online lessons

📱 Confident English (B2): free

Every class has an online lesson with the same grammar, phrases, and model conversations. It's your homework, and it helps you catch up if you miss a class. It works on a phone, a tablet, or a computer.

rays-esl.netlify.app/curriculum-b2/
  1. Open the website, or scan the QR code with your phone camera. 📷
  2. Choose the lesson your teacher gives you (for example, Lesson 3.2).
  3. Press the 🔊 buttons to hear the model sentences and conversations. Then shadow them: say each line along with the audio, copying the rhythm, the weak forms, and the stress. (No sound? Turn up the volume, or try the Chrome browser. The 🎧 Audio control lets you choose a US or UK voice and slow it down.)
  4. Do the exercises and the quiz, then try the speaking project. Record yourself on your phone, listen back, and write one thing to improve in your learning journal.

⏱ Try 30–40 minutes, 3 times a week. That's more useful than one long session. 🌟

The online lessons and our classes both use American English (apartment, line, practice, color). If you learned British English (flat, queue, practise, colour), that's fine too: both are correct, and noticing the differences is a B2 skill in itself!
