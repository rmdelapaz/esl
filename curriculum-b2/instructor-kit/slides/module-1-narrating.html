1.1 · Lead-in · flat vs layered

Same facts. Which is a story?

Flat
I arrived at the station. The train left. I waited two hours.
Layered
By the time I arrived at the station, the train had already left. I'd been rushing all morning, and now I was standing on an empty platform with two hours to wait.
What extra information do we get? (What happened before, how long, the scene, the feeling.)
1.1 · Four tenses · four jobs

Every tense has a job in the story

TenseFormJobExample
Past simpleverb + -ed / irregularmain eventsShe opened the door.
Past continuouswas / were + -ingbackgroundThe rain was falling.
Past perfecthad + past participlean earlier eventSomeone had broken the window.
Past perfect continuoushad been + -inghow long, beforeThey'd been arguing for hours.
SAY IT I'd been waiting /aɪd bɪn ˈweɪtɪŋ/ · 'd + been / pp = had · 'd + base verb = would
1.1 · Simple or continuous? · when or while? · check the meaning

had done · had been doing

had donefinished · a numberShe'd written three emails.
had been doingthe activity · how long · the causeShe'd been writing all night.
statesknow · be · have · wantI'd known him for years.
❌ had been knowing
"His eyes were red. He'd been crying." Crying at the story moment? (No, before.) Long or short? (Long.) Why red eyes? (The crying.)

"While I was cooking, the phone rang." Longer action? (Cooking.) The interruption? (The phone: when + past simple.)
DON'T OVERDO IT After I finished, I left. ✔ (after already shows the order)
1.1 · Model · an anecdote

"You'll never guess what happened…"

A: I'd been working on a big report all afternoon, and I was just finishing the last page when the power went out.

B: Oh no! Had you saved it?

A: I hadn't saved it for two hours! I was sitting there in the dark when my coworker came over. She'd been having the same problem for weeks.

B: And?

A: By the time the lights came back on, she'd already found my report. I was so relieved!

1.2 · The third conditional · flip reality

If + had done, … would have done

RealityThird conditional (imagined)
I didn't check the weather. We got soaked.If I had checked the weather, we wouldn't have gotten soaked.
You didn't tell me. I didn't help.If you'd told me, I'd have helped.
She studied medicine. She met him.If she hadn't studied medicine, she would never have met him.
"If you'd told me, I'd have helped." Did you tell me? (No.) Did I help? (No.) Can we change it? (No, it's finished.)
WATCH If I would have known ❌ · would of ❌ → would've /ˈwʊdəv/ ✔

1.2 · Mixed conditionals · two times, one sentence

Past cause → present result (and back)

TypeFormExample
(a) past → nowIf + had + pp, … would + baseIf I'd gone to bed earlier, I wouldn't be so tired today.
(b) always → pastIf + past simple / were, … would have + ppIf I were more organized, I wouldn't have forgotten the meeting.
"If I'd taken that job, I'd be rich now." When was the job? (Past.) Am I rich now? (No.) Why would be? (The result is now.)
WATCH …I would have been rich now ❌ → I would be rich now ✔
1.2 · Groups of 4 · 18 min

⛓️ Chain of Consequences

Start: Monday, 7 a.m.: Ana's alarm didn't go off.

S1: If Ana's alarm had gone off, she wouldn't have missed the bus.

S2: If she hadn't missed the bus, she wouldn't have walked through the park.

… …and if she hadn't tasted those tacos, she wouldn't be running a food truck today!

Your link starts with…? (The last result, as an if-clause.) · Turn the ending card when? (After 4 links.) · The last link? (A mixed conditional: now / today.) · Real classmates? (No! Card people only.)
1.3 · One trick: a past form = unreal

I wish… · If only…

Nowwish + past simpleI wish I had more time. (I don't.)
The pastwish + past perfectI wish I'd set an alarm. (I didn't.)
Annoying!wish + someone else + wouldI wish the printer would work!
StrongerIf only = wish, more emotionalIf only I hadn't eaten the whole pizza!
"I wish I had bought that car." Did I buy it? (No.) Now or past? (Past: a regret.) · "I wish I would go to the gym"? (No! → I wish I went to the gym more.)
1.3 · Past form, present meaning

would rather · it's (high) time

meI'd rather walk than wait. · I'd rather not.
someone elseI'd rather you didn't call after ten. (now / later)
already doneI'd rather you hadn't told him.
due nowIt's time we left. · It's time to go.
overdue 😤It's high time they fixed the elevator. · It's about time…
WATCH I'd rather you don't ❌ · It's time we leave ❌ · I'd rather to stay ❌ · I wish you will pass ❌ → I hope you pass ✔
