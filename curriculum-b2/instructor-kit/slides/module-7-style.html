7.1 · Spoken register · two voices, same request

Distance = politeness

Ana: Hey Sam, got a sec? Any chance you could swap shifts with me on Saturday?

Sam: Yeah, no problem.

Ana: You're a lifesaver!

Ana: Excuse me, Mr. Patel. Do you have a moment?

Mr. P: Of course. What can I do for you?

Ana: I was wondering whether it might be possible to swap my Saturday shift with Sam.

"I was wondering…": is Ana talking about the past? (No, now. It's softer.) · Is she sure the answer is yes? (No: "might," "possible" leave room for no.) · Say it to Sam? (Too stiff!)
7.1 · The formal email · five parts

Greeting → reason → detail → request → sign-off

① GreetingDear Customer Service Team, · Dear Mr. Lee:
② ReasonI am writing to complain about / inquire about / request…
③ DetailOn May 3, I purchased… · Unfortunately, the item…
④ RequestI would be grateful if you could… · I would therefore request…
⑤ Sign-offI look forward to hearing from you. · 🇺🇸 Sincerely, · 🇬🇧 Dear Sir or Madam → Yours faithfully · Dear Mr Lee → Yours sincerely
RULE One register per message · the purpose in the first line · calm, not angry
7.1 · Register Switch Workshop · groups of 3 · 22 min

Same message · three versions

  • A a text to a friend · B an email to a manager · C a formal letter to an organization (7 min)
  • Pass it on: check the four features and upgrade one line (4 min)
  • Read all three aloud in random order. Class: who's the reader? Which feature told you? (8 min)
  • Turn one version down the dial (3 min)
How many versions? (Three.) · Does the text need "Dear"? (No!) · Checking: rewrite everything? (No, one line.) · Say which is which? (No, the class guesses.) · Real boss's name? (No, use the card.)

7.2 · Listen & react · Anecdote Circle · 22 min

Stories are a two-person game

Priya: All of a sudden I'm absolutely soaked, head to toe. And this old man just looks at me and goes, "Lovely weather for it!"

Tom: No way! What did you do?

Priya: To cut a long story short, I got the job. And that's why I always carry an umbrella now.

  • Plan (key words): hook · scene · events · "…" · ending. 2 min each, token = your turn.
  • Listeners: two reactions + one follow-up question. 😮 No way! · 😬 Oh no! · ➡️ Then what?
Must it be true? (No: true, card, or invented.) · Full sentences on the planner? (No.) · Ask "Is that true?" (Never!)

7.3 · Signposts & argument · check the meaning

A listener can't scroll back

SignpostArgue
Today I'll cover…The main advantage is…
First, let's look at…There are two reasons for this…
Moving on to…Research suggests… / The figures show…
For instance… / Take, for example,…As a result,…
To sum up…So we're asking you to…
"Bike parking is better." Opinion or argument? (Opinion only.)
"…because 12 of us cycle and two bikes were stolen." (Argument: claim + reason + evidence.)
After a signpost? (Pause for a beat.)

7.3 · The Pitch Panel · teams of 3 · 22 min

Pitch for 2 minutes · answer for 2

  • S1 signpost + the problem · S2 solution + evidence + a technique · S3 concede–counter + close + call to action
  • Panel: ask from your objection cards, tick Persuasion Bingo, and vote (not for your own team!)
  • Every answer starts calmly: "That's a good question."
How long? (Two minutes.) · Everyone speaks? (Yes, one part each.) · Read a script? (No, key words.) · Where do the panel's questions come from? (The cards.) · "Bingo!" when? (At the end.)
