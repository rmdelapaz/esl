Teach Upper-Intermediate English to Adults, Ready to Run from Day One

Confident English (B2 Upper-Intermediate) is a complete 13-week classroom course for adults who have finished an intermediate course or placed at B2: workers, parents, and newcomers who already manage daily life in English and now need to present, persuade, negotiate, and write professionally. Every class is planned to the minute, with discussion and debate at its center. It works for a trained ESL teacher and for a well-prepared volunteer.

Why Programs Choose It

  • Zero prep to teach. Every class adds up to 90 minutes: warmer, presentation, drill, controlled practice, a real communicative task, feedback, and homework set-up. A teacher can open the guide and teach.
  • English for professional life. Learners practice what supervisors, clients, and colleges expect at this level: giving a structured presentation and handling questions, writing a formal email that gets a result, shifting register between a manager, a customer, and a friend, reporting precisely what was said in a meeting, and holding a debate with courtesy. It fits WIOA, workforce-readiness, and career-pathway goals.
  • Debate without division. Topics stay in everyday, workplace, and community territory, never politics or religion. Every opinion task offers an assigned side, so nobody has to reveal a personal view, and personal tasks always allow a "new identity" or an imagined story.
  • Accuracy and range. B2 learners often plateau: they're fluent but repetitive. Every lesson ends in real speaking, followed by delayed, targeted feedback on both correctness and the range of language used, with stretch tasks that push strong learners toward C1.
  • The grammar of nuance. Narrative tenses, third and mixed conditionals, regrets and preferences, verb patterns, past modals, impersonal passives, participle clauses, reporting verbs, cleft sentences and inversion, the future perfect, the causative, and word formation, taught through meaning and always put to work. Two review projects pull it together: a present-and-argue task at the halfway point and a final structured debate.
  • Listening for real life. A full lesson on fast, natural speech (weak forms, linking, gonna / wanna) and another on vague language (thirty-odd, seven-ish, sort of) prepare learners for the English they actually hear at work.
  • A free companion course. Every lesson mirrors the free online course Confident English (B2) (rays-esl.netlify.app/curriculum-b2) with 🔊 audio on model sentences and dialogues. It serves as homework and review, and lets learners catch up after a missed class. A free tools page shows learners how to translate lessons into their own language.
  • A clear path forward. The course is the fourth step of a complete free CEFR ladder (A1 → C2). It follows Independent English (B1), and learners who finish move into Advanced English (C1), with Business English and IELTS Academic Preparation available alongside.
