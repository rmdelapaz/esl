🏛️ Advanced English

A complete, self-paced C1 Advanced English course — the level of precision, register, and style. Twelve lessons take you into advanced aspect and modality, the subjunctive, inversion, nominalisation, cohesion, diplomatic hedging, phrasal verbs, and collocation — the finer machinery of truly fluent, sophisticated English.

📚 About This Course

This is real language study for the near-fluent. It builds directly on B2 and turns confident competence into command: the ability to express fine shades of meaning, control register from the diplomatic to the academic, and use the sophisticated structures and rich vocabulary that mark an advanced speaker.

It follows the CEFR C1 ("Effective Operational Proficiency") level: you can express yourself fluently and spontaneously without obvious searching for words, use language flexibly for social, academic, and professional purposes, and produce clear, well-structured, detailed text on complex subjects.

What You'll Be Able to Do

  • Use advanced aspect and modality to convey precise time and attitude
  • Command the subjunctive, inversion, and cleft/fronting for style and emphasis
  • Handle advanced and mixed conditionals and the full family of alternatives to if
  • Write and speak in a formal, academic register through nominalisation
  • Build cohesion with reference, substitution, and ellipsis
  • Hedge, soften, and be diplomatic — saying difficult things gracefully
  • Deploy phrasal verbs, collocation, and idiom with confidence and precision

Who This Course Is For

  • Self-study learners who have finished B2 and want to reach genuine advanced proficiency
  • Classroom students — every lesson has extended discussion, presentation, and writing-into-speaking tasks
  • Teachers looking for a ready-made, sequenced C1 syllabus
  • Learners preparing for Cambridge C1 Advanced (CAE) or IELTS ~7–8

💡 This is the deep end — make sure B2 is comfortable

C1 assumes fluent B2 grammar and a broad vocabulary. If B2 still feels effortful, consolidate with Confident English (B2) first; this course moves fast and prizes nuance over novelty.

🧭 How to Study This Course

  1. One lesson at a time, in order. C1 structures interlock tightly; later lessons draw on earlier ones.
  2. Prioritise nuance over coverage. Don't just learn a form — learn when and why a speaker chooses it over a near-synonym.
  3. Do the project. Each lesson ends with an extended speaking, writing-into-speaking, or discussion task — the crucible for advanced range.
  4. Read and listen widely and analytically. Notice these structures in quality journalism, essays, talks, and fiction, and steal them.
  5. Build a phrasebook. At C1, vocabulary is king — collect collocations, idioms, and phrasal verbs, and use them deliberately.

🧠 A word before you begin

At C1, progress is measured not in what you can finally say, but in how well — with what precision, grace, and appropriateness. The gains are subtle, the plateau is real, and the reward is a voice that sounds unmistakably accomplished. This is craftsmanship now. Let's refine it. 🌱
