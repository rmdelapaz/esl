7.1 · How fluent listeners listen

Read the blueprint, not every brick

  • A talk has a shape: preview → points → recap
  • The speaker tells you the shape as they go
  • Missed a word? Let it go. Rejoin at the next signpost
  • Main ideas come round twice: the recap is your safety net
You missed one word in point two. Disaster? (No.)
What do you do? (Listen for the next signpost.)
"This talk falls into three parts." What do you draw? (1 · 2 · 3, with space.)

Stops the second time? (No.)
Sentences or keywords? (Keywords.)
Examples in the summary? (No.)

7.2 · Thought groups · pause at meaning

Where you pause changes what you mean

"Let's eat / Grandma." vs "Let's eat Grandma."
(An invitation… or an alarming suggestion.)

"My brother / who lives in Rome / is a chef." How many brothers? (One.)
"My brother who lives in Rome / is a chef." (More than one.)
When the meeting finishes / I'll send you the notes / and we can talk tomorrow.

Pause after "the" to breathe? (No: pause at a joint, after a clause, before but / and / because.)
7.2 · The tonic · stress the new · contrast

I didn't say she took it.

Meaning
A Someone else said it, not me.
B I deny saying it.
C I implied it, but didn't say it aloud.
D Someone took it, but not her.
E She did something with it, but didn't take it.
F She took something, but not this.
"Where's the report?" "It's on your DESK." Why not stress "report"? (It's given: old information.)
"No, the RED one." What did the other person think? (Another color.)
Listen: which meaning, A–F?

7.2 · Freer task · 15 min

🎙️ The Delivery Clinic

  • Threes: speaker · coach · listener
  • Mark up 60–90 seconds: CAPITALS · / · ↘ ↗ ↘↗
  • Speaker delivers · coach follows the page and ticks the checklist
  • Listener (no page): the main point in one sentence
  • Coach: one thing that worked + one change, demonstrated
Does the listener see the page? (No.)
How many changes? (One.)
Does the coach talk about accent? (No: stress, pauses, tunes.)
How long? (60–90 seconds.)
7.3 · Turn-taking · building · checking

In · hold · over · build · check

Come inCould I just come in here ↗? · Sorry to jump in, but…
HoldIf I could just finish my point… · Just to finish my thought…
Hand overWhat are your thoughts, Priya ↘? · Does anyone see it differently?
Build onBuilding on what Anna said… · That ties in with Tom's point…
CheckWhen you say "flexible," do you mean…? · So if I understand you correctly, … ↗?
"Could I just come in here?" Question or order? (A question: the speaker can say "Let me just finish.") · Why say "Priya"? (It draws her in and shows you're tracking everyone.)
7.3 · Respectful challenge · check the meaning

Acknowledge → soften → challenge

"No, that's wrong."
Stronger view? (No, only a higher temperature.)

"I take your point about speed ↘↗, but I'm not sure we've thought through the cost ↘."
Agrees at all? (Partly: about speed.) Weak challenge? (No: the cost, clearly.)
  • Up to a point, yes, but…
  • I'd question whether…
  • Isn't there a risk that…?
  • Where we do agree is…
WATCH I am agree · discuss about · According to me → In my view
7.3 · Model · a seminar in action

The four-day week

Dana: I think a four-day week would boost productivity. What are your thoughts, Ben?

Ben: Building on what you said, several trials found output holding steady.

Priya: Could I just come in here? I take your point about productivity, but I'd question whether it works for a client-facing team.

Dana: So if I understand you correctly, your worry is coverage?

Marco: So where does that leave us? Maybe we trial it for two months?

Dana: So, to sum up, we broadly agree on a trial. Shall we move on to who'd own it?

7.3 · Freer task · 10 + 15 min

🗣️ The Seminar

  • Facilitator: opens · hands over by name · refocuses
  • Summarizer: half-time summary + final summary
  • Challenger: tests both sides · acknowledges first
  • Contributors: argue the side on the card
  • Observer: silent · tallies moves · feedback at the end
  • 4 move cards each: play one face up when you use it
Your own opinion? (No, the card.)
When does the summarizer speak? (Half time and the end.)
Before every challenge? (Acknowledge.)
All cards down: keep arguing? (No, only hand over.)
