Teach Advanced English to Adults, Ready for University and the Workplace

Advanced English (C1 Advanced) is a complete 13-week classroom course for adults who have finished an upper-intermediate course or placed at C1: professionals, university-bound learners, and experienced workers whose English is already fluent and now needs to be precise, academic, and professionally persuasive. Every class is planned to the minute and built around a demanding task: a seminar, a writing lab, an editing workshop, a presentation, or a defense. It works for a trained ESL teacher and for a well-prepared volunteer.

Why Programs Choose It

  • Zero prep to teach. Every class adds up to 90 minutes: warmer, input and analysis, focused practice, a demanding main task, feedback, and homework set-up. The skills lessons (critical reading, essay writing, note-taking, seminars) are planned as workshops. A teacher can open the guide and teach.
  • Academic and professional readiness. Learners practice what universities and employers expect at this level: reading an argument critically and naming its weaknesses, structuring an essay with a defensible thesis, writing reports, professional emails, and cover letters, following a lecture and taking structured notes, and holding their own in a seminar or meeting. It fits bridge programs, pre-university pathways, professional re-licensing, and WIOA career-pathway goals.
  • Writing labs that work. Every writing lesson ends in a real text, reviewed with a clear peer-review protocol and edited for concision. The course closes with a position paper that learners present and defend under questioning.
  • Precision, nuance, and register. Advanced future forms, degrees of certainty, the subjunctive, mixed conditionals and the alternatives to if, inversion, clefts, nominalization, cohesion, hedging and diplomatic English, phrasal verbs, collocation and connotation, participle clauses, dependent prepositions, and academic vocabulary, taught as choices and always put to work.
  • Accuracy, range, and appropriacy. C1 learners often plateau: fluent, correct, and still not quite right for the situation. Every lesson gives delayed, targeted feedback on all three, with stretch tasks that push strong learners toward C2.
  • Debate without division. Topics stay in workplace, education, technology, health-habits, community, and everyday-science territory, never party politics or religion. Every argument task offers an assigned side, so nobody has to reveal a personal view, and personal tasks always allow a "new identity" or an imagined example.
  • A free companion course. Every lesson mirrors the free online course Advanced English (C1) (rays-esl.netlify.app/curriculum-c1) with 🔊 audio on model sentences, dialogues, and a lecture. It serves as homework and review, and lets learners catch up after a missed class. A free tools page shows learners how to translate lessons into their own language.
  • A clear path forward. The course is the fifth step of a complete free CEFR ladder (A1 → C2). It follows Confident English (B2), and learners who finish move into Mastery English (C2), with IELTS Academic Preparation and Business English available alongside.
