Certificate of Completion

A landscape certificate for any course in the series. Click a line on the certificate and type (learner name, dates, hours, your program), or choose a course from the toolbar menu, then print one copy per learner. Or print it blank and fill it in by hand.

Any level A1–C2 · IELTS · Business Letter landscape Type-in or handwrite
Tips

Print on card stock or heavy paper, in color. Typed text stays on this page only until you reload, so print each certificate right after typing the name. Completion means the learner took part and met the course's own end-of-course bar (for example, the A1 final assessment), not a perfect score. Give the certificate in person at the last class; for many adult learners it's the first certificate they've received in years, or ever. If a learner prefers, print only a first name or a chosen name.
