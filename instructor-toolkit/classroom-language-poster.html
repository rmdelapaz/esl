Classroom Language Posters

Two printable posters for the classroom wall, one per page. "Classroom English" gives learners the phrases they need to keep learning when they're lost. "Learning English at home" shows them how to study between classes. Both are written in simple A1 English, so they work at every level.

2 posters Letter / A4 · 1 page each All levels
Teaching the classroom-language poster

Put it up in the first class and teach it: model each phrase with a gesture, drill it chorally, then act "lost" and let learners rescue you with the right phrase. After that, point to the poster instead of answering in another language. For extra impact, print at 129% on Tabloid/A3. (This note and the header above don't print.)

Classroom English

Use these phrases in class. It's OK to ask!

🔁
Can you repeat that, please?
🐢
Can you speak more slowly, please?
🤷
Sorry, I don't understand.
❓
What does        mean?
💬
How do you say        in English?
🔤
How do you spell it?
✏️
Can you write it, please?
📖
Which page?
✋
I have a question.
👥
Can we work together?
⏰
Sorry I'm late.
🚻
Can I go to the restroom, please?

Learning English at home

Small steps every day. You can do it!

🔊

Listen and repeat

Use the 🔊 Listen buttons in the online course. Listen, stop, and say it out loud.

⏱️

A little every day

15 minutes every day is better than 2 hours once a week.

🗣️

Say it out loud

Read your lesson out loud. Talk to yourself in English when you cook or walk.

📓

Keep a word notebook

Write new words with a picture or a word in your language.

📱

Record yourself

Record your voice on your phone. Listen. Try again.

🏷️

Label your home

Put notes on things: door, fridge, window. Read them every day.

📺

Watch and listen

Watch videos, shows or the news in English, with English subtitles.

👨‍👩‍👧

Practice with people

Say hello to neighbors, co-workers and your children's teachers in English.

😊

Mistakes are OK!

Every mistake helps you learn. Keep going.
