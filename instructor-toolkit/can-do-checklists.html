Can-Do Checklists, A1 to C2

"I can…" checklists that learners fill in themselves. English Foundations (A1) has one checklist for each of its 8 modules. A2, B1, B2, C1 and C2 each have one page, organized by skill. The language gets harder as the level goes up, so every learner can read their own page.

A1: 8 module checklists A2–C2: 1 page each 👂 📖 🗣️ ✏️ 4 skills Learner-facing
How to use them

When: at the end of each module (A1), or at the start, middle and end of a course (A2–C2). Learners tick one box per line. Use a different color pen each time, and the page becomes a record of progress. Model it first: do two lines together on the board and show that "Not yet" is a normal, honest answer. It's what the course is for. Low literacy: read each line aloud, act out the example, and let learners tick; or do it as a one-to-one chat. The statements are CEFR-aligned but written for this course. They aren't official CEFR wording.
