Class Register and Progress Tracker

One printable record for a whole course: an attendance grid for 24 classes, a score sheet for placement, progress tests and the final assessment, a can-do tick grid for each module, notes, and a one-page learner profile to copy for each learner.

Using the tracker with any course

The grid fits the 24-class plan (12 weeks × 2 classes of 90 minutes) used by the level kits. 12-lesson courses (IELTS, Business English): use classes 1–24 if you teach each lesson over two classes, or only 1–12. More than 16 learners: print a second copy. In the A1 kit, class 13 is Progress Test 1 and class 24 is the final assessment and celebration. Shade those columns in your own copy.

ESL Instructor Toolkit · Learner profile · Confidential

Learner Profile

Fill in with the learner at the start of the course, in a quiet one-to-one moment, using a shared language or an interpreter if that helps. Update it at the mid-point and at the end.

Privacy: every personal question is optional

Ask, don't require. A learner can skip any question or give a "new identity" answer. Never record immigration or legal status, reasons for leaving a country, or health details beyond what affects learning (for example, "needs large print"). Keep completed profiles locked away or in your program's secure system. Use them only for teaching, show the learner their own profile if they ask, and destroy them according to your program's policy when the course ends.
